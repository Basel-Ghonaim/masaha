# ADR 0019 — The brand: an ink for actions and a spark for what matters now

> **Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-11-12

## Context
The first identity, *Sea*, gave one petrol teal every role: actions, links, focus, hover and the selected state alike. Hover and selected looked the same, and on a card the hover was barely seen. The owner wanted a warm colour. Every warm primary tried (coral, a sea sunset) failed beside the destructive red: they differed from it by hue alone, and under colour blindness they looked the same.

A measured trial in the design archive compared two ink-and-spark candidates beside *Sea*, in both themes, on real screens: contrast, OKLab distance from the status colours, with and without simulated colour blindness. The logo was drawn with it, and the owner chose colour and logo together.

## Decision
- **The brand has two roles.**
  - An **ink**, a dark slate, carries actions, links, focus and structure. Links in running text are underlined, since colour alone no longer marks them.
  - A **spark**, a berry, marks what matters now, sparingly: as a fill, an edge or a mark. It is never small text, never on an action, and never destructive.
- **Hover is a neutral role of its own; selected is the spark's tint.** They are told apart by hue, and by a second mark where an item can be both (an edge, a bar).
- **The neutrals are drawn in the ink's hue.** The status colours are unchanged.
- **One brand in two themes,** light and dark.
- **The logo** is «مساحة» over "MASAHA" in the ink, the ة's two dots in the spark; the small mark is the ة with its dots. The name «مساحة / Masaha» is kept.
- **The trial's criteria are kept as tests,** so a later colour must meet them too.

## Alternatives
- **Keep *Sea*.** Rejected: one colour for every role is the problem itself.
- **A warm primary** (coral, the sea sunset). Rejected: too close to the destructive red, and the same under colour blindness.
- **Slate and coral** instead of berry. Equal by the measures; berry was chosen with the logo. Coral stays in the design archive for a later comparison.
- **A third theme, coral in dark.** Rejected: dark is where coral is nearest the error colour, and one brand in two themes is enough.
- **Hover as before, the muted fill.** Rejected: barely seen.

## Consequences
- Every screen changes at once, in both themes.
- Links in running text carry underlines.
- The colours that live outside the page, such as the browser's icons and the e-mails, carry the identity by hand, and tests hold them to it.
- A new spark must pass the measured criteria: contrast on its grounds, and its distance from the destructive red.
