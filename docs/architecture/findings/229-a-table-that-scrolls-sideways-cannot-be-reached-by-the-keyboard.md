# 229. A table that scrolls sideways cannot be reached by the keyboard

**Status:** Resolved · **Date:** 2026-11-22

**Evidence:** axe-core in Chrome on the owner's space profile, at 320 px (#150's pass, 2026-11-18, by its worker and by the supervisor): `scrollable-region-focusable` on «الأسعار المعلنة»'s table, its container `div[data-slot="table-container"]`. The layer's `Table` (`apps/web/src/shared/design-system/components/data/Table/Table.tsx`) scrolls its table sideways in that container (`overflow-x-auto`), and nothing in it takes the focus, so the keyboard cannot scroll it. The rule is the layer's, not the page's: every table drawn with `Table` — the prices (the space profile, the admin's space editor, Packages & prices), the public space page's hours, every `DataTable` — scrolls in the same container. On `main` before #150 too, outside [finding 224](224-the-maps-tooltip-and-two-scrolling-strips-fail-axe.md).

**Resolves when:** a `Table` that overflows its place is a named tab stop, and axe reports no `scrollable-region-focusable` on a table at 320 or 390, in either theme and language.

**Resolution (2026-11-22, #152):** the `Table` scrolls in the layer's `ScrollRegion` ([foundation §12](../../frontend/design-system/foundation.md#data)): a group and a tab stop only while the table overflows. `Table` takes exactly one name, a label or the element that names it, which names the table and its group alike, so a caller writes it once; the prices and the hours, named by a visually hidden caption until now, take it as their label, as `DataTable` already did. The focus ring is drawn inside the container, since the frames around a table clip what overflows them. Component tests prove the name, the tab stop and its absence while the table fits. axe-core in Chrome on `masaha_dev`'s demo data, Arabic and English, light and dark: the public space page's hours as a guest at 320 and 390, the owner's profile «الأسعار المعلنة» at 320 and 390, and the customers' `DataTable` at 390 (cards) and 768 (its table) report no violation; the prices' table, the one that overflows (at 320), is a named tab stop there, its ring whole.
