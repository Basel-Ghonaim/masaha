# 84. A person cannot work at two spaces' front desks

**Status:** Open · **Date:** 2026-10-18

**Evidence:** an owner creates new accounts only ([ADR 0009](../decisions/0009-space-scoped-reception-role.md), revised, #90): a receptionist who already has an account — at another space, or as a customer who registered — cannot be added; they would need a second account under another email.

**Resolves when:** a second owner can link an existing account on a proof the person gives in person (a code shown on their own signed-in device), or the owner decides v1 needs none.
