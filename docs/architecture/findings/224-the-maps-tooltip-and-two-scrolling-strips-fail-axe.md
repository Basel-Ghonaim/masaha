# 224. The map's tooltip and two scrolling strips fail axe

**Status:** Resolved · **Date:** 2026-11-12

**Evidence:** axe-core run in Chrome on every screen of the identity item's visual pass (2026-11-12, light-ar-390 and dark-en-1440). `aria-tooltip-name` (serious): the tooltip beside a selected mark on the directory's map, Leaflet's `role="tooltip"` element bound in `apps/web/src/shared/map/components/LeafletMarkerMap.tsx`, has no accessible name. `scrollable-region-focusable`, at 390 px: the strip of the other photos in `apps/web/src/features/spaces/components/photos/PhotosView.tsx` (`overflow-x-auto`) and the occupancy heatmap's card content (finding 223's) scroll with no focusable element inside, so the keyboard cannot scroll them. No colour-contrast violation was reported on any screen.

**Resolves when:** axe reports neither rule on those screens, in either theme.

**Resolution (2026-11-18, #150):** a region that scrolls sideways is the design system's: its new `ScrollRegion` ([foundation §12](../../frontend/design-system/foundation.md#display)) is a named group, a tab stop only while its content overflows, with the one focus style. The photos' phone row sits in it, named once (its list keeps its role, unnamed), and so does «ساعات الذروة»'s heatmap alone, named by its card's heading, the empty state outside it. A mark's tooltip on the map is hidden from assistive technology whenever Leaflet adds it, and the mark loses the `aria-describedby` Leaflet gives it ([architecture §6](../../frontend/architecture.md#6-map)). Component tests prove the names, the tab stop and the hiding. axe-core in Chrome on `masaha_dev`'s demo data, the owner's space, at 390 and 1440, light and dark, Arabic and English: no `aria-tooltip-name` and no `scrollable-region-focusable` on the map with a mark chosen or hovered, on Finance › Occupancy, or on the profile's «الصور», where `main` reported them; no other rule on those screens. The demo space has four photos, whose row fits at 390, so it was also checked at 320: its strip is a tab stop there and passes; the one rule left at 320 is the prices table's container, a place this finding does not name.
