# Desk

> **Status:** Active · **Class:** Description — what is built, as the code shows it · **Last Updated:** 2026-10-27 · **Owner:** Basel Ghoneim
> **Authority:** The `desk` capability: the front desk's orchestrations, each across the desk's modules in one transaction. Its endpoints are owned by the API contract ([Front desk (managed)](../api/api-contract.md#front-desk-managed)); its rules by [conventions › the front desk](../backend/conventions.md#the-front-desk-desk) and [§13](../backend/conventions.md#13-idempotency-and-concurrency). The API is built; on the web, the desk's screen («مكتب الاستقبال»): who is present, a visit's check-in and check-out with its payment; «الزبائن», the customers list, and a customer's file; and the owner's void of a payment, in «الدفعات»'s rows and a subscription's statement.
> **Scope:** the API module `desk` (L5); `@masaha/shared/desk`; the web feature `features/desk`, dashboard-only: the desk's screens.

## What it does

- Checks a visitor in, with what the check-in warns of: the space full, outside its hours, or announced closed.
- Answers a retried check-in the visit its idempotency key already made.
- Checks a subscriber in on one of their subscriptions, unless archived or present, with what it warns of: the space full, outside its hours, announced closed, the subscription not started or expired, this week's days or today's hours used up; a retry answers the same check-in and the same warnings.
- Checks a subscriber out, answered with what their subscription owes now; one closed already is answered as it is.
- Leaves the stays it does not close to the auto check-out, which [`visits`](visits.md) and `subscriptions` run as timed jobs.
- Checks a visit out: the charge at the quote's time, the payment taken, the rest left unpaid to a customer found or created by phone, and a price typed by hand where the visit has no rate, or an unpriced close; a retry answers the first result.
- Receives a payment for a closed visit after its check-out, once per idempotency key; the rest left unpaid by the check-out's rule. Or a payment on a subscription of the space.
- Leaves an uncollected visit's debt to a customer by phone, with no payment («تسجيل دينًا»).
- Writes off, as the owner, what a closed, priced visit still owes, with a reason, and undoes it.
- Voids a payment, as the owner, with a reason; a second void answers it as it is. On the web, the owner voids one from «الدفعات» ([payments](payments.md)) and from a subscription's statement.
- Archives a customer, as the owner, unless they are present.
- Subscribes a customer, from a package of the space or terms typed by whoever may type a price, once per idempotency key; renews one of their subscriptions, at most once; allocates an amount given over their debts, oldest first, the rest to the new subscription; warns while an older debt remains.
- Answers the customers list, paged, searched and filtered by their subscription and payment state, a customer's file, and a subscription's statement.

## Who uses it

- **The space's owner and reception,** through the API. The admin never reaches it: the desk is private from the admin ([ADR 0002](../architecture/decisions/0002-authorization-model.md)). On the dashboard, the owner and reception work «مكتب الاستقبال» (2026-10-24, #116). On the shared desk device, «تبديل الموظف» signs the person out on the server and opens sign-in with the desk as the page to return to; the server ends a reception session left idle for 15 minutes (#118), and the sign-in page then says why ([auth](auth.md), #122).

## Responsibility boundary

- **Owns** the orchestration: the transaction, its order, the space's lock and `can()` on the links read under it, and the retry's lookup before any write.
- **Leaves** the visit, its rates, its charge and its audit to [`visits`](visits.md); the payment and the ledger to [`payments`](payments.md); the customer found or created by phone, and the archive's column, to [`customers`](customers.md); a package's terms to [`packages`](packages.md); the subscription and its audit to [`subscriptions`](subscriptions.md); the warnings to [`occupancy`](occupancy.md); the lock to [`spaces`](spaces.md); the settings to [`space-settings`](space-settings.md); who may act, and who may type a price, to `can()` ([security › Authorization](../backend/security.md#authorization)).

## How it composes the platform

- Its manage router sits below `/manage/spaces/:spaceId`, behind the one boundary that admits the space's staff ([security › Authorization](../backend/security.md#authorization)).
- Each write asks `can()` at the route, before the body is read: `attendance.record` (the staff's) for the check-in and the check-out, and `payments.record` (the staff's) for the payment, each then reading its `Idempotency-Key`, and for «تسجيل دينًا», which needs none; `visits.writeOff` (the owner's) for the write-off and its undo; `payments.void` (the owner's) for a payment's void; `customers.archive` (the owner's) for a customer's archive; `subscriptions.manage` (the staff's) for subscribing, then its `Idempotency-Key`. The service asks `can()` again under the space's lock.

## Behaviour and flows

**The check-in.** In one transaction, in this order: the space's lock; `can()` on the links as they are under it; the warnings as of now; the visit this key already made, for a retry; else the visit, with its rates (through `visits`). Answered 201 every time, the warnings in `meta` only when there are some.

**A subscriber's check-in.** In one transaction, in this order: the space's lock and `can()` (`attendance.record`); the check-in this key already made — on another subscription → 409, else a retry; the subscription's row (`FOR UPDATE`, through `subscriptions`), not the space's → 422 `invalid_choice`; its customer archived → `CUSTOMER_ARCHIVED`; present by `occupancy`'s *present* → `MEMBER_ALREADY_CHECKED_IN`; the warnings, `occupancy`'s then `subscriptions`' (`limitWarnings`: the state by `subscription_usage`'s counts, and whether today is counted already); then the check-in, audited by `subscriptions`. A retry's limit warnings leave the check-in itself out (its day unless another shares it, and its minutes), so it answers what the first request did. Answered 201, with the customer and the subscription's state as of now.

**A subscriber's check-out.** In one transaction: the space's lock and `can()` (`attendance.record`); the check-in's row (`FOR UPDATE`, through `subscriptions`), not the space's → 404; closed already → answered as it is; else closed now, but never past the stay's due time, by the space's week and settings read under its lock, `MANUAL`, audited by `subscriptions`. Answered with the subscription's paid and balance (`payments`), so a usage-based subscriber's desk sees what the stay cost; the payment is the payment route's.

**The check-out.** In one transaction, in this order: the space's lock and `can()`; the settings; the visit's row (`FOR UPDATE`), not the space's → 404; the payment this key recorded. A visit already closed answers as it is, with that payment for a retry, and records nothing; closed by another key, a request with a payment is refused (`VISIT_ALREADY_CLOSED`). Else the time it is priced at (the quote's, at most five minutes old and never before the check-in; absent, now), but never past the stay's due time, which then closes it; the charge from `visits`; with no rate, the typed price of whoever may type one (`can()`'s `prices.typeByHand`), who must, or none for whoever may not: an unpriced close, which the owner prices later through [`visits`](visits.md). Any part left unpaid follows the one unpaid-part rule (below). Then the visit is closed, and the payment recorded, which the ledger checks; its refusal undoes the whole check-out. The rows are taken in one order: the space's, the visit's, the customer, the payment.

**The unpaid-part rule** (one helper, for the check-out and the payment). A visit with a customer keeps it and needs no phone; a phone that is not its customer's is refused (`VISIT_HAS_CUSTOMER`). A visit with none needs a phone, whose customer is found among those not archived, or created with the name given, else the visit's.

**The payment after the check-out.** In one transaction, in this order: the space's lock and `can()` (`payments.record`); the visit's row (`FOR UPDATE`), not the space's → 404; the payment this key recorded, for a retry, answered with the visit and the sums as they are now; a key that paid another item → 409; an open visit → 409. Then the payment is inserted, which the ledger checks (written off, unpriced, above the balance); then what the visit was paid and still owes; if anything remains, the unpaid-part rule, and the visit's customer set when it changes. The rows are taken in this order: the space's, the visit's, the payment, the customer.

**A subscription's payment** (the same route, `subscriptionId` instead of `visitId`). In the same transaction, after the space's lock and `can()` (`payments.record`): the subscription's row (`FOR UPDATE`), not the space's → `invalid_choice` on `subscriptionId`; the payment this key recorded, for another item → 409, for this one a retry, answered with the sums as of now; then the payment, which the ledger checks against a fixed price. Answered with the subscription's state, what was paid and what it owes (below zero while a usage-based one holds credit). An ended subscription, or an archived customer's, still takes one.

**«تسجيل دينًا».** In one transaction, in this order: the space's lock and `can()` (`payments.record`); the visit's row (`FOR UPDATE`), not the space's → 404, still open → 409; written off → `VISIT_WRITTEN_OFF`; nothing owed → `VISIT_ALREADY_PAID` (an unpriced visit has a debt to leave); then the unpaid-part rule; the visit's own customer again → the visit as it is; else its customer is set, audited `visit.leftToCustomer`.

**The customers list** (conventions §9, composed reads). `subscriptions` resolves the status, kind and package to ids (`NONE`: the ids to leave out); `payments` the payment state (`SETTLED`: those who owe and those in credit, left out); `customers` searches and pages what is left, by name; then, for the page's ids only, each one's current subscription and their debt and credit. `status=ARCHIVED` lists only the archived, and only for the owner (`can()`'s `customers.archive` on the loaded links). Read without a lock.

**A customer's file.** The customer (404 when not the space's); their subscriptions' states (`subscriptions`); their items' balances, debt and credit (`payments`' one query); who of them is present (an open check-in, else an open visit; `occupancy`'s *present*); and the unpaid visits — closed, not written off, owing or unpriced — with their visits (`visits`) and payments, voided ones marked (`payments`), the oldest first. Read without a lock.

**A subscription's statement.** The subscription's state (404 when not the space's), with its money from `payments`' one query; its customer; its check-ins (`subscriptions`), each with a closed stay's whole minutes; its payments, voided ones marked (`payments`); and the renewal's proposed start (`proposedStart`) while `subscriptions` names no renewal, else who renewed it. Read without a lock.

**The archive of a customer.** The owner's, in one transaction, in this order: the space's lock and `can()` (`customers.archive`); the customer, the space's (else 404); one already archived answered as it is; one present — an open check-in or an open visit, by `occupancy`'s *present* ([occupancy](occupancy.md)) — refused `MEMBER_ALREADY_CHECKED_IN`; then archived through `customers`. A customer who owes is archived: the debt stays theirs, and the web asks first (DK-014).

**Subscribing.** In one transaction, in this order: the space's lock and `can()` (`subscriptions.manage`); the subscription this key already made, for a retry, answered as of now; the customer, the space's (else 404) and not archived (`CUSTOMER_ARCHIVED`); the terms — a package of the space that is offered (else `invalid_choice` on `packageId`), copied, its end `validityDays` after its start, both days included; or typed, by whoever may type a price (`prices.typeByHand`, else `PRICE_TYPING_NOT_ALLOWED`), with its reason, its end as sent and never before its start; the subscription it renews, locked, the customer's own (else `invalid_choice` on `renewalOfId`) and not renewed yet (`SUBSCRIPTION_ALREADY_RENEWED`), read before the terms; the start date, the one sent, else the renewal's proposal (`proposedStart`), else today; then the subscription, through `subscriptions`; then the payment, if any: the customer's items that still owe, read under the lock, allocated oldest first by `payments`' `allocate`, one payment per part under the key `derivedKey(key, index)`, each checked by the ledger (above a fixed new price: `PAYMENT_EXCEEDS_DUE`, the whole request undone). A retry finds the subscription by its key, then its payments by the 50 keys it may have derived, in one query. Answered 201 with its state, what was paid and what it owes, the payments, the customer's debt, and `CUSTOMER_HAS_DEBT` in `meta.warnings` while an older item owes. The rows are taken in this order: the space's, the renewed subscription's, then each paid item's, oldest first (the ledger's trigger).

**The write-off and its undo.** The owner's, each in one transaction, in this order: the space's lock and `can()` (`visits.writeOff`); the visit's row (`FOR UPDATE`), not the space's → 404, still open → 409 with no code. The write-off: a visit already written off is answered as it is; no charge → `VISIT_NOT_PRICED`; then what it was paid and still owes, read from [`payments`](payments.md) under the visit's lock; nothing owed → `VISIT_ALREADY_PAID`; else `visits` writes it off with the amount, now, by the caller, with the reason. The undo: a visit not written off is answered as it is; else `visits` clears it, audited with what it was.

**A payment's void.** The owner's, in one transaction, in this order: the space's lock and `can()` (`payments.void`); the payment's row from [`payments`](payments.md) (`FOR UPDATE`), not the space's → 404; voided already → answered as it is, its first reason and voider kept, writing nothing; else `payments` voids it, which the ledger refuses for a written-off visit's (`VISIT_WRITTEN_OFF`). Two voids at once void it once; a void waiting while its visit is written off is refused.

**The desk on the web** (`features/desk`, composed by `pages/dashboard/space/desk/`). The page shows [occupancy](occupancy.md)'s people present and live status control, with this feature's «خروج» in each row, and «تسجيل زيارة» in the top bar from 768 px and in the bar at the foot below it.
- **The check-in** asks the visitor's name, «طالب» only where the terms carry student rates for the chosen shift, and «الوردية» only where the space has shifts, the current one on Gaza's clock chosen; it says the rate the visit will copy, from `visit-terms`, and, while the space is full, that it may still be checked in. It is sent at once: the server's warnings (`SPACE_FULL`, `OUTSIDE_OPENING_HOURS`, `CLOSURE_IN_FORCE`) are said after, in the toast, which then stays until it is closed.
- **The check-out** reads the visit's quote on opening and each minute while open, and reads it again before sending when it is older than 4 minutes; a changed due stops the send and says the new one. It sends the quote's time and the amount recorded, never more than the due. The money block fills «المستلم الآن» with the due and follows it until a figure is typed, and says one of four facts under it: paid in full, the change, the credit, or what stays owed and by whom. A remainder on a visit with no customer asks a name and a phone, which stay once shown. A visit with no rate asks a typed price and its reason of whoever may type one (`mayTypePrice`); whoever may not closes it unpriced, with a phone, the whole left owed. The toast says what the answer recorded: the amount and its method, or nothing paid, or unpriced, and what stays owed.
- **One intent, one key:** each opening of the check-in, the check-out or «تحصيل» holds one `Idempotency-Key`, kept across the transport's re-sends, a second press and «إعادة المحاولة» ([architecture §7](../frontend/architecture.md#7-server-state)). A lost answer says a retry is safe; a refusal says nothing was recorded, before its reason.
- **«غير محصّل»** lists what `…/uncollected-visits` answers, filtering nothing: the auto check-out's visits, and a visit closed by hand with no customer that owes again after a void. Its title counts the visits from the page's `meta`, never a sum of money; it shows three rows, then «عرض الكل» opens the page whole with its pages, 20 a page; arriving at `#uncollected` opens it and takes the focus to its heading. Each row says who, how and when it closed, and what it still owes, the server's: «تحصيل» while it has a price, «تسجيل دين» while it has no customer, and the owner's «لن يُحصَّل…» in its «⋯».
- **«تحصيل»** fills the money block with what the visit still owes and records at most that, with its key; a remainder on a visit with no customer asks a name and a phone, as at the check-out. **«تسجيل دين»** asks the name first, then the phone, and leaves the whole rest to that phone's customer. **«لن يُحصَّل…»** asks first, says what a write-off is and is not, and requires its reason; its toast stays until it is closed, with «تراجع», which undoes it once. No screen lists a written-off visit after it ([finding 164](../architecture/findings/164-a-written-off-visit-is-listed-nowhere.md)).
- **The one search** (K1), above the page, from 768 px focused on arrival: once typing pauses (400 ms), the people present whose name, or whose phone's last two digits or more (a subscriber's, from the open check-ins), answer it — «حاضر الآن», [occupancy](occupancy.md)'s, with their «خروج»; then the first six customers the customers list's search finds (`GET …/customers?q`), those present left out, a card each — the name, the phone, the current subscription with its status and its end, and the balance — under «زبائن»; then «تسجيل زيارة باسم «X»» («تسجيل زيارة» for digits), which opens the visit's check-in with the name typed. The count of results is said politely as it changes; a failed search says so with a retry, never as no match.
- **A subscriber's «حضور»** on a customer's card: on a current subscription that runs (active, or ending soon) one tap checks them in, with one key until it lands; on none, an expired one or one not started — what the row already says — a choice first: «تسجيل كزيارة», a visit by their name that is not added to their file ([finding 160](../architecture/findings/160-a-visits-check-in-cannot-name-its-customer.md)), and «تسجيل على أي حال» on the subscription (not with none). A coming subscription's start is read from the customer's file. The server's warnings are said after, in the toast; `MEMBER_ALREADY_CHECKED_IN` says they are here.
- **A subscriber's «خروج»** is one tap with no key, in the present list and in the search's first group: the toast says how long they stayed and what their subscription still owes, from the answer. The open check-ins (`GET …/check-ins?open=true`), read again whenever the state now's check-ins change, give each subscriber's phone to the search and their subscription's first progress line to the present list's usage cell. After a check-in or a check-out started from the search, it is cleared and takes the focus.
- **«استلام دفعة»** — in the top bar from 768 px (in its «⋯» below it), on a customer's card as «استلام {المبلغ}» while they owe, and in a subscriber's check-out toast while the subscription still owes — records one payment on one item. Opened with no customer, it lists the customers who owe (`payment=OWES`), those present now first, searched by name or a phone's last digits; with one, it reads their file and offers its open items — the unpaid visits, the oldest first, an unpriced one left out, then the subscriptions that owe — the one the entry named chosen, else the first. The money block fills «المستلم الآن» with what the item still owes and follows the item only while untouched; a usage-based subscription takes more, as credit, and may be paid when it owes nothing. The opening holds one key; the toast names the customer, the item and what it still owes, from the answer.
- **«الزبائن»** (`pages/dashboard/space/customers/`) lists the space's customers, 20 a page, from `GET …/customers`: the search (once typing pauses), the status chips — «الكل», the four statuses, and «مؤرشف» for the owner only — and the kind and the payment, every filter and the page in the address ([customers › Web decisions](customers.md#web-decisions)). From 768 px a table of five columns — the name, a link to the customer's file, with the phone under it; the current subscription with its end; its status, or «مؤرشف»; the balance's badge; the actions — and a card per customer below it, where the kind and the payment move into a sheet with how many apply. The count is said politely; the list's states: loading, failed with its reference and a retry, failed again over the rows (they stay), no customers yet, none matching, no archived customer; while another page or filter loads the rows stay, marked busy.
- **«ملف الزبون»** (`…/customers/:customerId`, both roles) is titled by the customer's name once their file (`GET …/customers/:customerId`) has loaded, under «الزبائن» › the name. Its header: the phone, «حاضر الآن» while present, the current subscription's status (the one running, else the next, else the latest), the balance's badge, and «مؤرشف». Then «زيارات غير مدفوعة», each row as «غير محصّل» draws one, «تحصيل» opening «استلام دفعة» on it (an unpriced one says «بدون سعر بعد»); then «الاشتراكات», a card each in a grid of one, two, then three columns: the name with «سعر يدوي» when typed, its dates, its terms in one line, its status and payment badges (paid, partly paid, unpaid), its progress, and its due («المستحق حتى الآن» for a usage-based one), paid and left — the server's — with «استلام المتبقي» while it owes. A customer the space does not hold is its own state.
- **A subscription's statement** opens in its card with «عرض الكشف» (`aria-expanded`), read then (`GET …/subscriptions/:subscriptionId`), never before: its check-ins — the day, in and out on Gaza's clock, the stay, «حاضر الآن» while open, an auto check-out marked —, a row each that stacks below 768 px; its payments, the amount with its method under it, a voided one struck through with «ملغاة» and «أُلغيت: {reason}» — no name of who recorded or voided it ([finding 162](../architecture/findings/162-no-route-names-a-spaces-staff-to-reception.md)); and the renewal's proposed start. Its sums and lists agree: the server reads them in one snapshot (finding 124).
- **«إنهاء الاشتراك»** on a card not ended nor expired asks first, in an `AlertDialog`, naming the end it will take — today, or its start if not started — and the end it had, and that the amounts do not change; it is sent once with no key, and the file is read again. A subscription's corrections (its limits, dates, name or price) have no screen ([finding 163](../architecture/findings/163-a-subscriptions-corrections-have-no-screen.md)).
- **A customer's «⋯»** — on each row of «الزبائن», each card of the search and the file's header — holds «ملف الزبون» (not in the file), «تعديل البيانات» and the owner's «أرشفة…»; an archived customer shows the owner «استرجاع» instead ([customers › Web decisions](customers.md#web-decisions)).
- **A payment's void on the web** (the owner's): a payment's «⋯», named «إجراءات الدفعة: {المبلغ} · {الاسم}» (DI-9), holds «إلغاء الدفعة…», destructive; its `AlertDialog` (DI-7) names the amount, who, the day and the method, says «تبقى ظاهرة مشطوبة مع السبب واسمك، ولا تُحسب في الدخل ولا في التسليم. لا يمكن التراجع.» and asks the reason, required, focused first (the dialog places that focus itself: an alert dialog's own goes to «رجوع», [finding 172](../architecture/findings/172-the-write-offs-reason-is-not-focused-first.md)). It is sent once with no key (`POST …/payments/:paymentId/void`): no answer says «لم يصل الرد. أعد المحاولة — إلغاؤها مرة ثانية لا يغيّر شيئًا.»; `VISIT_WRITTEN_OFF` says «لا تُلغى دفعة زيارة شُطب مبلغها.» (DI-12), a 404 that the payment no longer exists, and a 403 that it is no longer open here. Done, the space is read again and the toast names the amount and who. Closed, the focus returns to the «⋯»; once the «⋯» leaves under the focus, the page places it (DI-8): in a statement, on its payments' heading. The payments page sets it in each row of the ledger; a statement's payment not voided offers it to the owner (S10).
- **«بدون سعر»** lists the visits closed with no charge — unpriced at the desk, or by the auto check-out with no rate — only for whoever may type a price (`visit-terms`' `mayTypePrice`); for anyone else it is not even read, and the card is absent with none. Its title counts them all; it shows the first page, each priced leaving it. «تحديد السعر» asks the amount and its reason, both required, in the check-out's words, and is sent once; `PRICE_TYPING_NOT_ALLOWED` reads the terms again, so the card goes.

## Decisions

### API decisions

- **A desk write takes the space's lock first, and decides `can()` on the links read under it.** *Why:* a reception link ended meanwhile must refuse ([finding 43](../architecture/findings/43-two-races-around-a-spaces-facts.md)); the lock also serialises the space's desk creates. 2026-10-20, #95.
- **A retry is found by its key first, under the space's lock; the unique key is only the backstop.** *Why:* inside a transaction PostgreSQL aborts on the unique violation, so the first record could not be read after it; under the lock the read is race-free ([conventions §13](../backend/conventions.md#idempotency)). 2026-10-20, #95.
- **A package's terms are copied into the subscription when it is made; the package is offered (not retired) and the space's.** *Why:* a subscription is a snapshot ([ADR 0010](../architecture/decisions/0010-manual-payment-ledger.md)): a later edit of the package changes no subscription made from it. 2026-10-22, #106.
- **The warnings never refuse; a retry answers them as of now, the visit counted present.** *Why:* the desk decides; the warnings describe now, not the visit, so storing them would add a column for advice — ADR 0015's "same body", weakened for them only (the owner's answer, §13 Q8 of the desk's plan). 2026-10-20, #95.
- **The key is read from the `Idempotency-Key` header by a route middleware, before the body.** *Why:* a request without one is a client's bug, refused before anything is read. 2026-10-20, #95.
- **The check-out is priced at the desk's quote, at most five minutes old and not in the future.** *Why:* with per-minute rounding the charge moves every minute; a visitor who paid what the screen said must not owe a few agorot more when the request lands, and five minutes cannot back-date a stay (the owner's bound). 2026-10-20, #95.
- **The customers list and file are composed here, every filter resolved to ids before the page is cut; their reads ask no `requireCan`.** *Why:* a screen that filters by other modules' values is composed above them (conventions §9), and every page must be full with the total right (§5); a read is the staff's, so the boundary is their check (#86). Archived customers are the owner's to list. 2026-10-21, #102.
- **A customer who is present is not archived (`MEMBER_ALREADY_CHECKED_IN`); one who owes is.** *Why:* an archived customer present at the desk could not be checked out from its list; a debt stays owed whoever is on file (the plan's S11; the owner's settled rule). The archive is the desk's because it asks who is present, across `subscriptions` and `visits`. 2026-10-21, #102.
- **A stay checked out after its due time is closed at that due time, its `checkedOutAt`, and charged up to it; the audit keeps the request's own time.** *Why:* every stay is due by 23:59 of its check-in's day, so a stay never crosses into the next day on any path, the job's or the desk's; the record's times then agree with its charge and with what the auto check-out would have recorded, while `requestedAt` says when the desk closed it ([finding 116](../architecture/findings/116-a-desk-check-out-of-an-overdue-stay-is-charged-up-to-now.md)). 2026-10-21, #102.
- **A subscriber's stay checked out after its due time is closed at that due time too, by the one rule the visit's check-out uses (`spaces.stayEnd`); the audit keeps the request's own time.** *Why:* the subscriber's check-out closed at now, so an overnight stay counted every hour to the morning in the subscription's minutes and due, which no route corrects; one rule for both stays, so the two cannot drift (#109's review). 2026-10-24, #110.
- **A visit with no rate, checked out by whoever may not type a price, closes unpriced; whoever may type must.** *Why:* never free, never blocked at the door; the owner prices it later (the owner's answer to Q4, built here rather than after an interim refusal, alternative A1). 2026-10-20, #95.
- **A part left unpaid needs a phone, whose customer is found among those not archived, or created with the name given, else the visit's.** *Why:* a debt always has someone to collect from (data model › Unpaid visit); a found customer keeps the name they have. 2026-10-20, #95.
- **A visit that has a customer keeps it, at the check-out and at a later payment; a phone that is not its customer's is refused (`VISIT_HAS_CUSTOMER`).** *Why:* one rule for an unpaid part, in one helper; another phone would move a customer's debt to someone else ([finding 100](../architecture/findings/100-an-unpaid-check-out-moves-a-visits-customer-to-the-phones.md)). The phone is compared with the visit's customer's own. 2026-10-20, #100.
- **The payment after the check-out is its own route in `desk`, `POST …/payments`, its body naming the visit.** *Why:* the unpaid-part rule lives here, beside the check-out; the body mirrors the ledger's one item, so step 8 adds `subscriptionId` without breaking a client. 2026-10-20, #100.
- **The payment is inserted before the customer is attached.** *Why:* the ledger's refusals (written off, unpriced, above the balance) answer their code before a phone is asked for, and no service repeats them; a 422 on the phone after the insert rolls it back. 2026-10-20, #100.
- **«تسجيل دينًا» is its own `PUT …/visits/:visitId/customer`, not the payment route with no amount.** *Why:* a `POST …/payments` that records no payment would answer "created" for nothing and spend a key; setting a visit's customer is idempotent by design (the same phone finds the same customer under the space's lock), so it needs no key. 2026-10-20, #100.
- **A retried payment answers the first payment, with the visit and the two sums as of now.** *Why:* the desk shows what remains on a weak connection; the sums describe now, as a check-in's warnings do (the owner's answer, Q6 of the plan). 2026-10-20, #100.
- **A check-out of a visit already closed answers it as it is and records nothing; a key that paid another visit is a `conflict`.** *Why:* updates are idempotent by design ([conventions §13](../backend/conventions.md#idempotency)); a reused key is a client's bug. 2026-10-20, #95.
- **A check-out that carries a payment, of a visit another key already closed, is refused (`VISIT_ALREADY_CLOSED`).** *Why:* two desks, or a slow connection, could otherwise each take the cash and only one be recorded; a 200 with `payment: null` reads as success while the money is in no ledger (the owner's decision on #95's review). 2026-10-20, #97.
- **A check-out time before the visit's check-in is refused (`out_of_range`).** *Why:* the database refuses a visit closed before it opened, which would answer 500. 2026-10-20, #97.
- **The write-off is a sub-resource: `POST` writes it off, `DELETE` undoes it; a second of either answers the visit as it is.** *Why:* a repeated write is idempotent by design, as a repeated check-out is, so it needs no key ([conventions §13](../backend/conventions.md#idempotency)); the first reason is kept. 2026-10-20, #100. A delayed retry of one can land after the other and flip the state back; accepted for v1, provided the desk's screen never retries these two routes automatically ([finding 115](../architecture/findings/115-a-write-off-and-its-undo-carry-no-key.md)). 2026-10-21, #102.
- **The balance is read under the visit's lock, and nothing owed is refused (`VISIT_ALREADY_PAID`).** *Why:* two desks can collect the last part while the owner writes the visit off; the one that waits decides on what was committed. 2026-10-20, #100.
- **The subscribe takes a published week or month price beside a package and custom terms; reception may use it, nothing being typed.** The price is read from `spaces` by its key, in the subscribe's transaction. *Why:* reception subscribes from what the space publishes (the owner's §14.4). 2026-10-25, #118.
- **A statement and a file are each read in one `REPEATABLE READ` snapshot.** *Why:* their sums and their lists must agree ([finding 124](../architecture/findings/124-a-statements-sums-and-its-payments-come-from-different-snapshots.md)). 2026-10-25, #118.
- **An unpriced visit is refused with the payment's own code, `VISIT_NOT_PRICED`.** *Why:* one rule — a visit with no price is priced first — so one code (the owner's answer, Q2 of the plan). 2026-10-20, #100.

### Web decisions

- **`desk` on the web holds the desk's screens** (S1): it calls the desk module's routes and those of `visits` the same staff call on the same screens (the terms, the quote); the state now and the override are [occupancy](occupancy.md)'s, and the page sets one in the other's slot. *Why:* a feature per module would put every dialog that reads one module and writes another in the page. 2026-10-24, #116.
- **The desk sends a write once, beyond the transport's rule** (S3): only a keyed write is re-sent by the transport; the override is sent once, and a lost answer is the user's to retry. 2026-10-24, #116.
- **One key per intent** (S4): a dialog's opening makes its key; a retry, a second press and an edit of the form keep it; a new opening is a new key. *Why:* ADR 0015 rule 1; a key per press would record twice on a double press over a weak connection. 2026-10-24, #116.
- **The warnings are the server's, said after** (S6): a check-in is never held for a warning the browser would have to work out a second time. 2026-10-24, #116.
- **Money is agorot end to end, and the due is the server's** (S7): the web computes only the line under the money block — recorded, change, what stays — never a charge. 2026-10-24, #116.
- **Every line after a write is worded from the answer** (S8), never from the form. 2026-10-24, #116.
- **The keyboard at speed** (S10): each dialog's first field takes the focus («المستلم الآن», or the typed price with no rate); Enter submits; when the person whose «خروج» had the focus leaves the list, the focus goes to the next row's «خروج», else to the list's heading. 2026-10-24, #116.
- **Two failure lines, two facts** (DI-8): no answer — «لم يصل الرد. أعد المحاولة — لن تُسجَّل مرتين.»; a refusal — «لم يُسجَّل شيء.» before its reason. 2026-10-24, #116.
- **A typed price carries its reason** (DI-10), «سبب السعر», required, as the contract requires. 2026-10-24, #116.
- **«لم يدفع شيئًا» goes both ways** (DI-12): at nothing received it reads «استلام المبلغ كاملًا» and gives the due back. 2026-10-24, #116.
- **The debt's fields stay once shown** (DI-13), required only while something stays owed. 2026-10-24, #116.
- **Row actions are 44 px on touch** (DI-16): «خروج» takes the layer's default size, and the bar's «تسجيل زيارة» `size="lg"`. 2026-10-24, #116.
- **The due has its own weight** (DI-25), and **one money fact under the block** (DI-26). 2026-10-24, #116.
- **The unkeyed writes are sent once** (S3): «تسجيل دين», the write-off and its undo carry no key — each is idempotent against itself — so the transport never re-sends them and the desk never retries them by itself; a lost answer is the user's to retry ([finding 115](../architecture/findings/115-a-write-off-and-its-undo-carry-no-key.md)). 2026-10-25, #117.
- **The uncollected visits are counted, never summed** (DI-3): the list is paged and no answer sums it, so a page's sum would be false past 20. 2026-10-25, #117.
- **«تحصيل» is the row's primary** (DI-4); «تسجيل دين» outlined, the owner's «⋯» quiet, its «لن يُحصَّل…» destructive (DI-27). 2026-10-25, #117.
- **A write-off is reversible, and says so** (DI-9): «… ليست دفعة ولا حذفًا: تبقى في السجل مع السبب، ويمكن التراجع عنها من الإشعار.» 2026-10-25, #117.
- **A partial collection's rest needs a phone** on a visit with no customer (DI-11), as the contract requires: the rest becomes a customer's debt, never left uncollected with no one. 2026-10-25, #117.
- **The card is the overview's anchor** (DI-19, `#uncollected`), and **shows three rows, then all** (DI-23). 2026-10-25, #117.
- **The undo lives in the write-off's toast** (DI-29), which stays until it is closed: no read lists written-off visits. 2026-10-25, #117.
- **The focus returns to the control that opened a dialog** (DI-30): the row's «تحصيل», «تسجيل دين» or «⋯»; the row gone, the card's heading. 2026-10-25, #117.
- **One search for the desk** (K1): the people present first — the same state now, no request more — then the customers the server's search finds, then the visit; a customer present is in the first group only. *Why:* the receptionist's first question is whether the person in front of them is already here. 2026-10-25, #119.
- **Never two equal buttons** (DI-20): «تسجيل زيارة باسم «X»» is the primary only when nothing matched, outlined otherwise; and **the no-match line promises no record** (DI-36): «زائر لمرة واحدة؟ سجّله كزيارة (لا يُنشأ ملف زبون).» 2026-10-25, #119.
- **The groups outweigh their cards** (DI-38): «حاضر الآن» and «زبائن» are `h2`, a card's name its `h3`. 2026-10-25, #119.
- **The desk asks first only what the row already says** (S6, DI-14): no current subscription, an expired one, or one not started opens the choice; every other warning is the server's, said after the check-in. 2026-10-25, #119.
- **A one-tap check-out keeps the focus** (DI-31): its «خروج» stays focused and busy while it is sent, so the list hands the focus to the next row's «خروج» once the row leaves; from the search, the search takes it. 2026-10-25, #119.
- **A payment pays one item** (DI-22): the dialog lists the customer's open items and pays the one chosen, one payment per submit; no «الأقدم أولًا» across items — only a subscribe allocates. **The debtors present come first** in its picker (DI-21), and **switching items keeps a typed amount** (DI-35): the amount follows the item only while untouched. 2026-10-25, #119.
- **One balance badge** (DI-40): «عليه {n}» a warning, «له رصيد {n}» a success, «مسدّد» neutral, everywhere a customer's balance is shown. 2026-10-25, #119.
- **A file's subscriptions are cards under one heading** (DI-42): the file's sections `h2`, each card's name an `h3`. 2026-10-25, #122.
- **The file has no tabs** (DI-43): the unpaid visits, then the subscriptions; the API holds neither a customer's paid visits nor their attendance across subscriptions. 2026-10-25, #122.
- **A subscription's arrears are one tap away** (DI-44): «استلام المتبقي» on a card that owes opens the payment with that item chosen. 2026-10-25, #122.
- **A statement opens in its card, not in a tab** (DI-43), its payments' amounts with their method under them (VD-006), a voided one struck through with a visible «ملغاة» and its reason (AX-017); **no «سجّلها»** (DI-48): no route names the staff to reception (finding 162). 2026-10-25, #122.
- **The end is sent once** (S3): «إنهاء الاشتراك» carries no key, so a lost answer is the user's to retry. 2026-10-25, #122.
- **A customer's «⋯» says whose** (DI-39): its trigger and its menu are both «إجراءات أخرى: {name}»; **«أرشفة…» is destructive** (DI-46); **a present customer's archive says why** (DI-47). 2026-10-25, #122.
- **Reception's unpriced close says so** (DI-28): «لا سعر لهذه الزيارة. تُغلق بدون سعر، ويحدّده المالك لاحقًا.», and asks the phone the whole is left to. 2026-10-24, #116.
- **The subscription sheet** (screen 16, K3): one end sheet, full width on a phone, opened from every entry with what it knows — «زبون جديد» in the desk's and the customers' top bars, the search's «زبون جديد باسم «X»» (a link, never a second primary: DI-20) or «زبون جديد بهذا الرقم», a customer's «⋯» «اشتراك جديد». The page passes `packages`' offered packages and `spaces`' shifts (S1); each opening mounts a new sheet, so its keys are that opening's. 2026-10-26, #124.
- **No preview of the allocation** (decision 3, [finding 125](../architecture/findings/125-the-allocations-order-is-not-exposed-to-the-client.md)): the sheet lists what the customer owes from before, each item and the total from their file, and says «يُطبَّق المستلم على المستحق من قبل أولًا، ثم على الاشتراك الجديد.»; «المستلم الآن» starts at that debt plus the new price; the toast lists each part the answer's `payments` allocated, the price the server charged and the debt left, and `CUSTOMER_HAS_DEBT`'s line. *Why:* one owner of the rule, the server; the default amount pays every part. 2026-10-26, #124.
- **A new customer is two intents, two keys** (S4, DI-37): the customer is created, then subscribed; a subscribe refused after the customer was saved says «أُضيف {name} إلى الزبائن؛ لم يُحفظ الاشتراك.» with the reason, keeps every field, and «حفظ الاشتراك» sends the subscribe alone with its key; closing then says «{name} في الزبائن بدون اشتراك.». 2026-10-26, #124.
- **A package's terms are copied, never edited** (DI-50): a package is one line (DK-007) — its name, its price or rate, its validity and terms in a plain caption, no warning icon for no limit (DI-45); another price is «مخصّص». **«مخصّص» is offered only to whoever may type a price** (`visit-terms`' `mayTypePrice`, S5) and asks what the contract does (DI-52): a name prefilled «مخصّص», «لمن», the billing, the price or the rate, four optional limits, the shift where the space has any, an optional end, and «سبب السعر», required. 2026-10-26, #124.
- **The phone is asked only of a new customer** (DI-53), required while anything stays owed; a known customer with none gets a line, never a block. The start is typed, today by default; a package's end is said from its validity, both days counted, and never sent: the server sets it. 2026-10-26, #124.
- **The renewal starts where its statement says** (K3, DK-007): «تجديد» on the file's latest subscription opens the sheet, which reads that subscription's statement — `renewal.startsOn`, never a start of the browser's — offers its package as one line with «تغيير الباقة», or repeats its custom terms, and sends `renewalOfId`; a subscription renewed meanwhile is said (`SUBSCRIPTION_ALREADY_RENEWED`) and the sheet makes a new subscription instead. 2026-10-26, #124.
- **The published week and month prices are sold first** (spec D, DI-51): under «منشورة للزوار», «أسبوعي» / «شهري» (with «· للطلاب» and the shift's name), each sent as `published: { period, audience, shiftId?, name }` — the name the line shows, in the desk's language — never a price's id; reception sells them, nothing being typed. A custom-labelled price is not offered. 2026-10-26, #124.
- **A published price is read afresh, and charged as the server says** ([finding 171](../architecture/findings/171-a-published-price-changed-between-the-desks-read-and-the-subscribe-is-charged-silently.md), the supervisor's decision for the owner to confirm): the sheet reads the space's prices again each time it opens, never showing an older read; the toast states the price the answer charged; a subscribe refused for the price (`invalid_choice` on `published`, or `PAYMENT_EXCEEDS_DUE`) says so, keeps every field and reads the prices again. *Why:* the API compares no amount; the finding stays open for that. 2026-10-26, #124.

- **The void is the desk's on the web, set by the payments page in its rows** (S1): the void is the desk module's route, so `VoidPaymentAction` is `desk`'s, exported, and `payments`' ledger takes it through its row slot. *Why:* each capability's web feature calls its own module's routes (architecture §3). 2026-10-27, #127.
- **The void in a statement too** (S10): a statement's payment not voided offers the owner the same «⋯» (DK-016). *Why:* a payment recorded on the wrong subscription is found in its file first. 2026-10-27, #127.
- **A written-off visit's payment is not voided** (DI-12): the refusal says so; the write-off's undo lives only in its toast ([finding 164](../architecture/findings/164-a-written-off-visit-is-listed-nowhere.md)). 2026-10-27, #127.
