# Directory

> **Status:** Active · **Class:** Description — what is built, as the code shows it · **Last Updated:** 2026-11-10 · **Owner:** Basel Ghoneim
> **Authority:** The `directory` capability: what anyone reads without signing in, over the publicly listed spaces: the directory's list, the map's pins, a space's public page and its live status; and a signed-in user's favourites as its cards. Its endpoints are owned by the [API contract](../api/api-contract.md#directory-public) and [its favourites](../api/api-contract.md#favourites); "publicly listed", "verified" and the stale prices by the [data model](../architecture/data-model.md#derived-values-computed-not-stored); the location's rules by [security](../backend/security.md#location). On the web, the directory's list, its filters and near me, its map, a space's page, Home's spaces and its areas' counts, About's legend of the statuses, and the account's favourites are built.
> **Scope:** the API module `directory` (L5); `@masaha/shared/directory`; the web feature `features/directory`, for any page group.

## What it does

- Lists the publicly listed spaces a page at a time, filtered by area, price, student prices, open now, open in the evening, Friday, verified and a search, ordered by the latest update, by name, by best match, by the price of a subscription, or nearest first from a location the device sends; a card carries the subscription's price while one is chosen.
- Answers the map its pins: every space that passes the same filters, unpaged, up to a ceiling, within the map's viewport when it is given.
- Answers each subscription's lowest and highest price over the listed spaces, the budget's hints.
- Answers a space's public page: its profile, hours and shifts, prices, amenities, contacts and each group's freshness, and its running and upcoming announcements.
- Carries each card's and pin's live status and its state by its hours, and answers a space's two states alone.
- Answers a signed-in user's favourites whole, as its cards, newest saved first or nearest first from the device's location; one that left the directory by its name, `listed: false` ([favorites](favorites.md)).
- On the site's «المساحات», lists the spaces a page at a time, with a search, filters by place, price and state, and an order, or nearest first from the device's rounded location, each card a link to the space's page.
- On the site's «المساحات», shows the same spaces on a map, «قائمة | خريطة», each a pin whose live state is a shape as well as a colour, the chosen one's card leading to its page.
- On the site, shows a space's page at `/spaces/:slug`: its prices per audience, its hours with today, its amenities, its description, its place on a map with directions, its contacts, and each group's freshness; an unverified space's notice offers the platform's contact.
- On the site's «المفضّلة», shows a signed-in user's favourites as its cards, newest saved first or nearest first, and the spaces that left by their names.

The page shows its announcements: a running closure's notice at its top, the others after its status, both drawn by [`announcements`](announcements.md) in the page's slots.

## Who uses it

- **Anyone,** signed in or not, through the API, and on the site: «المساحات», the directory's list and map, and a space's page; on Home, its spaces and the areas' counts.
- **A signed-in user,** through the API: `GET /me/favorites`; on the site, the hearts read from it which spaces are saved, and «المفضّلة» (`/me/favorites`) lists them (2026-10-28, #129).

## Responsibility boundary

- **Owns** no table. It composes the reads below it, and owns the public payloads and their caching.
- **Leaves** the spaces' filters, the search's score, the orders and the pages to [`spaces`](spaces.md), which owns the tables they read, and the rule of the hidden prices.
- **Leaves** which areas are listed, and how a search matches an area's names, to [`lookups`](lookups.md); which spaces are verified to [`space-links`](space-links.md).
- **Leaves** the live status to [`occupancy`](occupancy.md): its rule, the state by the hours, and which verified spaces are closed by hand; which spaces a closure covers now to [`announcements`](announcements.md).
- **Leaves** which announcements the public sees, and their order, to [`announcements`](announcements.md).
- **Leaves** which spaces a user saved, and when, to [`favorites`](favorites.md); the names of those that left the directory to [`spaces`](spaces.md) (`namesWithDeleted`).

## How it composes the platform

- Its public router is mounted at the API's root, unguarded, behind the general rate limit ([security › Rate limits](../backend/security.md#rate-limits-fixed-window)).
- Its me router, at the API's root too, answers `GET /me/favorites`: `no-store` when the point is sent, then `requireAuth`, then the query with its header, by the list's rule (one function reads both).
- **A composed read** ([conventions › Composed reads](../backend/conventions.md#composed-reads)): each filter on another module's value is resolved to ids first, by its owner, in one query: the listed areas and the areas a search matches by `lookups`, the verified spaces by `space-links`, and with `openNow`, the spaces under a closure in force by `announcements` and the verified spaces closed by hand by `occupancy`. `spaces` then filters, by the hours too, searches, orders and pages ([conventions §5](../backend/conventions.md#5-pagination)); the areas' names, and on the page the amenities' names, follow, one query each, and the live status and the state by the hours of the spaces answered, set-based, from `occupancy`, from the weeks they were listed with. An answer reads the clock once: its filter and its states are of one instant.
- **The search's one rule** is the database's: a function normalises the names and the query alike, and another scores how well they match ([data model › Conventions](../architecture/data-model.md#conventions)).
- **The page** is read under the space's share lock, as the admin's read is ([conventions §13](../backend/conventions.md#concurrency)); whether it is verified is read from its links under the same lock, through `space-links`, with the facts it decides on.
- **The location** is read from its header with the query, by one shared schema, and kept off the request, so the request logger, which redacts the header, never writes it ([security › Location](../backend/security.md#location)).
- **On the web,** the site's Directory page sets the list's slots: the space's link (`publicSpacePath`), each card's area, named by [lookups](lookups.md)' `AreaName` from the public catalogue, and the place filter, `lookups`' `AreaFilter`. The filters are drawn once per width with the design system's `useMediaQuery`. Near me keeps its state in a store of the feature's own, in memory. The pages are `shared/lists`' `ListPagination`; the list is read with TanStack Query's app defaults, under `['public', 'directory']`.
- **A space's page on the web** is set by the site's page (`SpaceDetails`): its area, named by `lookups`' `AreaName`; the way back to the directory; and the platform's contact, [`platform-settings`](../frontend/architecture.md#3-capabilities-features)' `PlatformContact`, in an unverified space's dialog. Its map is `shared/map`'s `PlaceMap`; its carrier and date rules are `shared/lib`'s. The page is read once under `['public', 'directory', 'space', slug]` with the app's defaults, and titles the page with the space's name.

## Behaviour and flows

**The list.** Every filter applies before the page is cut, so every page is full and the total right; every order ends with the id, so a tie keeps its place across pages. With `q`, the best match comes first unless another order is asked. The card carries the names, the area, verified, the week, its cover's card-size URL, or none, its live status and its state by its hours, and with `period` its price (`priceAgorot`, the lowest general price of the period within the bounds); no freshness, capacity or count. The period alone filters (it always has: the web held it back until a bound was typed, 2026-11-08). `priceAsc` and `priceDesc` order by that price, which needs the period. `openNow` keeps the spaces open now by their hours on Gaza's clock, under no closure in force, a verified one not closed by hand (a full one stays); `evening` keeps those whose week has a day that closes at 20:00 or later; both are SQL before the page is cut, and a space without hours passes neither.

**Near me.** The device rounds its location to three decimals and sends it in the `Masaha-Near` header, only to sort by distance. Each space then carries its straight-line distance, to the nearest 100 m, and the answer is stored by no cache.

**The map.** The pins of every space that passes, unpaged; past 1,000, the first 1,000 by id, marked truncated, and the map asks again within its viewport. A pin carries what its selected card shows: the names, the area's id (named from the public catalogue), the place, verified, the week, the cover, the live status and the state by the hours.

**A space's page.** A space that is not publicly listed is not found. An unverified space's stale prices are hidden (`null`), a verified space's shown with the group marked stale. The amenities are one list: the platform's active ones in its order, with their icons, then the space's own. Its photos come in the gallery's order, the cover first, each in both sizes, read under the same lock as its facts. Its announcements follow, from `announcements`: running and upcoming ones, never an ended or a deleted one, by start then id, at most 20.

**A space's live status.** The status and the state by the hours alone, for a publicly listed space; anything else is not found, as its page. Whether it is verified is read from its links.

**The price ranges.** `GET /price-ranges` answers each period's lowest and highest general price over the listed spaces whose prices are shown, whatever the other filters, `null` for a period none prices.

**Caching.** Every answer may be kept for 30 seconds and revalidated with its `ETag`, except one ordered by distance.

**The directory's list on the web.** `DirectoryList` shows 18 spaces a page (one, two or three columns alike; lighter on a weak connection; 2026-11-08, #139). Each card is one link to the space's page, named by its name and described by its area, its status, «موثّقة», today's hours and, last, its price, so they are read after the name: the cover, or the space's mark (the shown name's first letter) when it has none or its cover fails to load, in a frame of fixed ratio; the name; the area with its governorate; a verified space's live status by its word, an unverified one's state by its hours, «مفتوحة الآن» or «مغلقة الآن», on a neutral badge, or «لا تتوفر حالة مباشرة»; «موثّقة»; today's hours on Gaza's calendar, or «ساعات العمل غير متوفرة»; while a subscription is chosen, its price, «15 ₪ / يوم», the amount left to right, worded by the subscription its answer was asked with, so while another subscription's page loads the cards shown keep their own. The search reads both names and the area's, and reaches the address once typing pauses; the order is «الأحدث تحديثًا» or «حسب الاسم», «الأفضل تطابقًا» while a search applies, and «الأرخص شهريًا» and «الأغلى شهريًا», named by the subscription, while one is chosen — absent, not disabled, under «الكل». The search, the filters, the order and the page live in the address. The count says the Strip's spaces, or the matching ones once anything narrows the list, worded from a real answer only: while the next one loads, it keeps its last line; a count of none draws no line, the empty state saying it. Loading shows skeleton cards; a failure says so, with its reference and «إعادة المحاولة» (too many requests counts down its wait, then offers it again); a failed refresh keeps the cards, the failure above them; an empty directory and no match (with «مسح الفلاتر») are their own states; a page past the last shows the last. While another page loads, the cards shown stay, the results marked busy; a page the visitor chooses takes the focus to the results' heading, while a search, an order or a page past the last, which replace the address, leave it where it is.

**The directory's filters on the web.** Top to bottom: the place (any number of areas, `lookups`' `AreaFilter`); the price, «السعر»: the subscription as one segmented choice («الكل · ساعة · يوم · أسبوع · شهر», the design system's `SegmentedControl`, named «نوع الاشتراك»), «الكل» by default with «اختر نوع الاشتراك لتحديد نطاق السعر أو الترتيب حسبه.»; once one is chosen, its budget «من» «إلى» in whole shekels, hinted by that subscription's lowest and highest price (`GET /price-ranges`, rounded outward to whole shekels), Arabic digits read, each refused bound said under its field and left out (0, letters, above 100,000, and «إلى» under «من», said on «إلى»), with «بالشيكل، على السعر الشهري.» under it; «الدوام», three checkboxes, «مفتوحة الآن», «مفتوحة مساءً» and «مفتوحة يوم الجمعة»; and «أخرى», two, «المساحات الموثّقة فقط» and «أسعار للطلاب». **Near me on the web.** «الأقرب إليّ» asks for the device's location only when pressed, never on load. The position is judged where it arrives: less precise than 2 km, or outside the Strip's box once rounded, it is not used; otherwise only its point rounded to three decimals is kept, in memory, never in the address or the browser's storage. ## Decisions

### API decisions

- **The list is paged and filtered on the server** (the owner, D1). *Why:* it must hold at a thousand spaces; it replaces the plan's "whole filtered set in one request". 2026-10-13, the directory item.
- **"Near me" sorts on the server, from a location sent in a request header, rounded on the device to three decimals, never stored or logged** (the owner, D2 and the reply's decisions 4 and 5). *Why:* paging needs the server to sort; a header reaches no URL a host or a proxy logs; at about 110 m, "near" keeps its meaning among close spaces while naming a block, not a home. It changes the v1 commitment that the location is never sent ([overview](../project/overview.md#public-site)). 2026-10-13, the directory item.
- **A finer point is refused, not rounded** (S3). *Why:* a client that skips the rounding fails loudly instead of sending a precise location. 2026-10-13, the directory item.
- **The map reads its own light pins, at `/map/spaces`** (D3, S1). *Why:* the map needs the whole set, and a slug could be `pins`. 2026-10-13, the directory item.
- **The map's ceiling is 1,000 pins, the rest asked within the viewport** (D3). *Why:* it holds at a thousand spaces in about 25 kB compressed. 2026-10-13, the directory item.
