# Space Links

> **Status:** Active · **Class:** Description — what is built, as the code shows it · **Last Updated:** 2026-10-20 · **Owner:** Basel Ghoneim
> **Authority:** The `space-links` capability: a user's links to spaces and their role at each ([ADR 0009](../architecture/decisions/0009-space-scoped-reception-role.md)), as the session, the caller's own list, the space switcher, the admin's spaces list and users list, the admin's owners (added, linked and unlinked), the owner's staff screen and the links loader use them. Its endpoints are owned by the API contract: [*Managed spaces*](../api/api-contract.md#managed-spaces), [*Owners (the admin)*](../api/api-contract.md#owners-the-admin) and [*Users (the admin)*](../api/api-contract.md#users-the-admin); the `SpaceManager` entity and "verified" by the [data model](../architecture/data-model.md#spaces).
> **Scope:** the API module `space-links` (L2); `@masaha/shared/space-links`; the web features `features/space-links` and `features/staff` (the owner's reception accounts, a second audience over the same module), both dashboard-only.

## What it does

- Gives the session a user's active links, with the role at each, oldest first.
- Lists the spaces the caller works at, with their names, area and role there.
- Shows the space switcher in a space's dashboard.
- Composes the admin's spaces list: its filters, its owners and its verified state, and on the API the soft-deleted spaces as a list of their own; and shows it on the dashboard's spaces page, its deleted spaces included, with its filters kept in the address.
- Loads a space's links for the routes on one space, so `can()` knows the caller's role there and whether the space is verified.
- Answers the [directory](directory.md) which spaces are verified, every one in one query: its filter, and the stale prices it hides.
- Composes the admin's users list, on the API: its filters, and the spaces each person owns and those at whose desk they work; the owners page is that list filtered to the owners, and shows it on the dashboard's owners page, with its search and its state kept in the address; the users page shows it whole, with its search, its role and its state kept in the address.
- Adds an owner for the admin, on the API: a new account with a temporary password, or an existing one upgraded on purpose, linked to one space; and from the dashboard's owners page.
- Links a space's owners for the admin, and ends their links, one space at a time, on the API; the person's global role follows their owner links; on the dashboard, from an owner's row, a person's row on the users page, and a space's editor.
- Changes a person's global role for the admin, between `USER` and `ADMIN`, while they hold no active link, on the API and from the dashboard's users page.
- Keeps a space's staff for its owner, on the API: creates a new reception account with a temporary password (never an existing account's email), lists the space's reception links whole, and deactivates (ending every session of the person) and re-activates them; issues a new temporary password while one is pending, and records each handover; on the dashboard's «الموظفون», lists them, adds one, removes and gives back their access, and issues a new temporary password while one is pending, each password shown once.

Reception accounts' management API access is built: an active `RECEPTION` link can read its
space and its photos, but cannot mutate profile, facts, confirmations or photos.

## Who uses it

- **Every signed-in user with links,** through the switcher and the session's links, which the dashboard's guards and landing read.
- **A space's owner,** on the dashboard's «الموظفون» (`/dashboard/spaces/:spaceId/staff`), through `features/staff`: their reception accounts.
- **The admin,** through the spaces list, on the dashboard's spaces page, the admin's routes on one space, the users list, on the dashboard's owners and users pages, and adding an owner.
- **`auth`,** which puts the links in every `Session` it builds.

## Responsibility boundary

- **Owns** the `space_managers` table, the role at a space, whether a space is verified (an active `OWNER` link), and the rule that the global `OWNER` follows a person's owner links ([ADR 0009](../architecture/decisions/0009-space-scoped-reception-role.md)), applied through `users`, which owns the role and the sessions' end.
- **Leaves** a space's facts, the listing's own filters and the pagination to [`spaces`](spaces.md), and a governorate's areas to [`lookups`](lookups.md); it asks each for ids or names, one query per module.
- **Leaves** the access decision to `can()` and the space middleware ([conventions › Space access](../backend/conventions.md#space-access)); no module imports `space-links` to learn its access.
- **Leaves** routing to the platform: the space guard, the landing and the last space are [frontend architecture §2](../frontend/architecture.md#landing-and-guards)'s. The switcher only navigates: the selected space is the URL's ([ADR 0016](../architecture/decisions/0016-dashboard-urls.md)).

## How it composes the platform

- **The caller's spaces** use the `me` router at `/manage/spaces`, behind `requireAuth` only: the path names no space, so the space middleware does not run.
- **The links loader** is `shared/auth`'s, wired with this module's links. On the admin's space routes it runs without its refusal ([conventions › Space access](../backend/conventions.md#space-access)).
- **Managed routes** take their access from these links: the boundary on `/manage/spaces/:spaceId` reads them through the links loader ([security › Authorization](../backend/security.md#authorization)).
- **The admin's spaces list** is a composed read ([conventions › Composed reads](../backend/conventions.md#composed-reads)). Each filter on another module's value is resolved to ids first: the verified state from the links, here, and a governorate to its areas, by `lookups`. `spaces` then applies its own filters (deleted, hidden, stale, the area, the search) to those ids and pages ([conventions §5](../backend/conventions.md#5-pagination)). The page's owners, their names (from `users`) and its areas' names follow, one query per module.
- **The single-space admin read** is supplied active owner names (`ownersFor`) by the admin composition here, while the list composition remains unchanged. The names are read from `users` in the read's own transaction, under the space's share lock.
- **A link change** is one transaction: it takes the space's lock through `spaces`; for an existing account, then the person's session lock through `users` (a new account's row is inserted under the space's lock), then counts the person's owner links and asks `users` to change the role when they give another ([conventions §13](../backend/conventions.md#concurrency)).
- **The admin's users list** is a composed read too: every filter is the account's own, so `users` filters and pages its accounts; the page's active links follow, of both roles, and their spaces' names from `spaces`, which leaves a soft-deleted space out, one query per module.
- **The web** reads through the transport and TanStack Query, the caller's spaces under the user's own key scope and the admin's list under the admin's ([architecture §7](../frontend/architecture.md#7-server-state)); the dashboard-only rule keeps it out of the site's download ([architecture §3](../frontend/architecture.md#3-capabilities-features)).
- **The admin's list on the web** is one section that the spaces page sets, with two slots it fills from other capabilities: each row's actions, from [`spaces`](spaces.md), and the place field, from [`lookups`](lookups.md) ([architecture › What a feature exports](../frontend/architecture.md#what-a-feature-exports)); and the path each space's name leads to, the space's editor (`spaceLink`). Its filters and page live in the address ([architecture › Where state lives](../frontend/architecture.md#where-state-lives)).
- **The owner's staff** use the managed router at `/manage/spaces/:spaceId/staff`, behind the boundary, every route asking `requireCan('staff.manage')` first; the creation counts under its limit before its body is read. Each write takes the space's lock (and `can()` on the links under it), then works through `users` for the account. The list is composed: the reception links here, their accounts from `users`, one query each.
- **The owners page on the web** reads the users list under `['admin', 'space-links', 'users', request]`, in the admin's scope, with `role=owner` fixed by its own hook; the section takes from the page only the path of the deleted spaces, where an owner whose every space is deleted is led.
- **The users page on the web** reads the same list under the same key, its role from the address. Its section takes from the page each row's `actions` and the path of the deleted spaces; the row menu is composed by the page from the actions this capability exports — `useUserLinkActions` for the link and role items, `useUserLinkDialog` for the open dialog's state, which the page holds above the list, and `UserLinkDialog` — and [`users`](users.md)' account items ([architecture › What a feature exports](../frontend/architecture.md#what-a-feature-exports)).

## Behaviour and flows

**The session's links** are the user's active links, oldest first, without those to a soft-deleted space. **The caller's spaces** are the same links with each space's slug, names and area, a hidden space included.

**The switcher** sits in the space's sidebar header. It shows the space in the URL, its name and area. For anyone with more than one active link, whatever their role at each, it opens their spaces, and choosing one follows the link the page gives it. A URL whose space is none of theirs offers their spaces. While the spaces load it shows a skeleton; a failure offers to try again, and a failed refetch keeps the list already loaded. The space's shell also reads the list for the space's slug, which its public page link needs.

**The admin's list** is composed as above, one page at a time; its rows are the [contract](../api/api-contract.md#spaces-the-admin)'s. Its `deleted` status lists the soft-deleted spaces alone, with the same filters, order and pages, each row naming its owners and when it was deleted, so a screen can restore one (`POST …/restore`) once the delete's Undo has gone; every other status leaves them out. On the web it is the list's fourth status, shown with the rest.

**Adding an owner.** An email with no account gets one, an `OWNER` with a temporary password, answered once, linked to the chosen space in the same transaction. An email with an account is refused, so the admin learns of it, and upgraded only when sent again with the upgrade asked: it keeps its name and password. An admin, a suspended account, an owner already (linked instead) and the space's active reception are refused.

**Linking and unlinking an owner.** Linking makes a user an owner, or gives an owner one more space; the space is verified. A suspension keeps the owner's links, so their space stays verified, and the admin edits it only after unlinking them ([security › Authorization](../backend/security.md#authorization)). Ending a link deactivates it; the last one leaves the space unverified and the person a user again. An admin, a suspended account and the space's active reception are refused; what each answers is the [contract](../api/api-contract.md#owners-the-admin)'s.

**The admin's role change** makes a person an admin or a user again. `OWNER` follows the links, so a person holding any active link, owner or reception, is refused until their links end. The change takes the person's lock with every active admin's row ([conventions › Concurrency](../backend/conventions.md#concurrency)), then counts their links under it; `users` writes the role, ends their sessions and audits it.

**The admin's users list** is composed as above, newest first, one page at a time; each row carries how the account signs in, when it joined, and the spaces of its active owner links, oldest first ([the contract](../api/api-contract.md#users-the-admin)).

**The admin's list on the web.** `AdminSpacesList` shows the search and the filters, then the page's spaces: a table from 1024 px (the name, a link to the space's page the page gives, the area, the state, the owners, the freshness, the last update and the row's actions), with the count the filters keep beside the search and the pages under the table; below it, a card per space with the same facts; on a phone, the search in view and the other filters in a bottom sheet behind a "Filters" button that counts the sheet's own two fields.
- **The filters:** part of a name, typed and written once typing pauses; one "Governorate / area" field; and the state, whose four choices are the contract's, «محذوفة» included. Each applies at once and returns to the first page.
- **The count and the chip:** the count the filters keep stands beside the search, at every width, and next to it a chip, «قديمة أو ناقصة», keeps the spaces with a stale or a missing fact group — the contract's `stale=true`, missing groups included, so its words say both. A filter is a chip, not a field (A7): it is in no sheet and no row.
- **The address** keeps them, `?q=&status=&governorate=&area=&stale=true&page=`, so a reload, a link or Back shows the same list. Each is read by the contract's own rule, so a value the server would refuse falls back to its default; an area wins over a governorate; the defaults are left out. A filter replaces the address; the pages are links. A page past the last, as after deleting its last row, shows the last page. A governorate or an area the address names by a valid id that no place has leaves the place field blank and the list empty, and "Clear filters" recovers it.
- **A row** names the space in the interface's language, an English-only name marked as English in the Arabic interface; its area; its state as two facts, its verification on a badge always — verified or unverified — and «مخفية» beside it when hidden, hidden no longer replacing the verification; its owners, or for none a dash read aloud as "No owner"; its freshness, the stale groups and the missing ones on a warning badge each, or "Up to date"; and its last update, the day and the month, with the year when it is not this one, on Gaza's calendar.
- **A deleted row**, under «محذوفة», is muted: its name is text and leads nowhere, since its editor would only say that it is gone; it carries no badge of its own, one muted line «محذوفة · …» by the same short date as the last update standing where its state was, and a dash, read aloud as nothing, where its freshness was — nothing will fill a deleted space's gaps. Its owners and its pages are the others'. Every other status leaves the deleted spaces out. Its one action, **Restore**, belongs to [`spaces`](spaces.md#behaviour-and-flows), and the way out of a deleted space to a user — restore it, unlink its owner, delete it again — is written there ([finding 50](../architecture/findings/50-an-owner-whose-only-spaces-are-deleted-stays-an-owner.md)); of the three steps, the restore and the unlink have screens, the unlink once the restored space shows in its owner's row again.
- **Its states:** placeholder rows while the first page loads; while another page or filter loads, the rows and the pages shown stay, the list marked busy, so a page chosen from the keyboard keeps the focus; a failure, with its reference and "Try again", which waits out a 429; "No spaces yet"; and, while a filter applies, "No spaces match the filters", with a button that clears them all. A failed refetch keeps the rows.
- **The focus:** when the row that held it leaves the list, as a deleted space's does, the focus moves to the list rather than being lost.

**The owners page on the web.** `OwnersList` is the users list with its role fixed to the owners (`GET /admin/users?role=owner`), under a line that says what linking an owner does. From 768 px a table: the owner's name, their email (under the name until 1024 px, its own column from there, left to right), their linked spaces (a neutral badge each, an English-only name marked as English in the Arabic interface) and their state, «نشط» on a success badge or «موقوف» on a neutral one; on a phone, a card per owner with the same facts.
- **The search and the state:** part of a name or an email, written once typing pauses; the state is chips, «الكل · نشط · موقوف», beside the count the filters keep, at every width — a single filter of three choices is chips, not a field in a sheet (P5, A7). A chip applies at once and returns to the first page; the chip already on stays on.
- **The address** keeps them, `?q=&state=&page=`, each read by the contract's own rule, so a value the server would refuse falls back to its default; the defaults are left out. A choice replaces the address; the pages are links. A page past the last shows the last page.
- **An owner whose every space is deleted** has no space in the row, since the list leaves a deleted space out, while their role still counts its link ([finding 50](../architecture/findings/50-an-owner-whose-only-spaces-are-deleted-stays-an-owner.md)): the row reads «كل مساحاته محذوفة — استرجع المساحة لفك ربطه», the whole line a link to the deleted spaces, where the restore begins the way out.
- **Its states** are the spaces list's: placeholder rows while the first page loads; the rows kept, marked busy, while another page or filter loads; a failure with its reference and "Try again", which waits out a 429; «لا يوجد أصحاب مساحات بعد»; and, while a filter applies, «لا يوجد أصحاب مساحات يطابقون البحث», with a button that clears them. When the row that held the focus leaves the list, the focus moves to the list.

**The users page on the web.** `UsersList` is the whole users list (`GET /admin/users`). From 1024 px a table: the person's name, their email (left to right), their role («مستخدم» · «مالك» · «أدمن»), how they sign in («كلمة مرور» · «Google» · «كلمة مرور وGoogle»), when they joined (the lists' short date), their spaces — «مالك» and a neutral badge per space they own, «استقبال» and one per front desk they work, an English-only name marked as English in the Arabic interface; a dash, read as «لا مساحات», for neither — their state, and the row's actions; below 1024 px, 768 included, a card per person: the name, the email, «{role} · {sign-in} · {joined}», the spaces, the state and the actions (A7).
- **The search, the role and the state:** the owners page's search; the role chips «الكل · مستخدم · مالك · أدمن» and the state chips «الكل · نشط · موقوف» beside the count, at every width, no sheet. The address keeps them, `?q=&role=&state=&page=`, each read by the contract's own rule; a chip replaces the address and returns to the first page. The owners page never reads `?role=`: its role is fixed, and a role in its address is neither asked for nor counted as a filter.
- **The admin's own row** reads «أنت» in place of its actions.
- **An owner whose every space is deleted** reads the owners page's line, a link to the deleted spaces ([finding 50](../architecture/findings/50-an-owner-whose-only-spaces-are-deleted-stays-an-owner.md)).
- **Its states** are the owners page's, in its words: «لا يوجد مستخدمون بعد» and «لا يوجد مستخدمون يطابقون البحث».
- **Each other row's menu,** «إجراءات: {name}», offers «تغيير الدور» first (below), then «ربط بمساحة»: the link dialog from the person, held for an admin («حساب أدمن — لا يُربط بمساحة») and a suspended account («الحساب موقوف — استرجعه أولًا»). A user's link says «ويصبح حسابه حساب مالك، ويُطلب منه الدخول من جديد.»; the person's own spaces read «مربوطة» and the spaces whose front desk they work «مكتب استقبال», neither to be chosen.

**The role change on the web.** «تغيير الدور» is offered to a user with no link and to an admin. A user: «جعل {name} أدمن؟», «يحصل على كل صلاحيات الإدارة على المنصة وكل مساحاتها، وتنتهي جلساته فيدخل من جديد.», «جعله أدمن»; an admin: «إعادة {name} مستخدمًا؟», «يفقد لوحة الإدارة فورًا، وتنتهي جلساته.», «إعادته مستخدمًا». Each opens on «رجوع», its action destructive and staying until the answer; while the request is pending, neither «رجوع» nor Escape closes it, so its answer is never lost. Changed, it closes once the admin's views have arrived again, and a toast names the person and says their sessions ended.
- **Held up front, its reason under it,** for every case the row can see: an owner, «دوره يتبع روابطه — فُكّ ربطه أولًا», with «فك روابطه» leading to the owners page searched by their email; an owner whose every space is deleted, the deleted-spaces line, with «المساحات المحذوفة» leading there; a receptionist, «يعمل في مكتب استقبال — يفكّه مالك المساحة أولًا».
- **What the row cannot see** is said by the server, in the dialog, which stays open: `ACCOUNT_HAS_LINKS` (a deleted space's link, or one made meanwhile), `ACCOUNT_PASSWORD_PENDING`, `LAST_ADMIN`, `OWN_ACCOUNT` and an account gone.

**Adding an owner on the web.** «إضافة مالك», above the owners list at its end, opens a sheet: the name, the email and one space, searched on the server (up to fifty, each option with its state badges and its owners). Before the act it says what will happen: «سيُنشأ حساب ويظهر رمز مرور مؤقت مرة واحدة — جهّز طريقة تسليمه.», and «إنشاء حساب وربط». `ACCOUNT_IS_ADMIN`, `ACCOUNT_SUSPENDED` and `ALREADY_OWNER` are worded on the form, which stays open.
- **An email with an account:** after `EMAIL_HAS_ACCOUNT` the sheet reads the email's account from the users list (`GET /admin/users?q=`) and shows whose it is — the name, when it joined, its spaces and its reception desks — with «تأكّد أنه الشخص المقصود: سيصبح مالكًا لهذه المساحة» and what the upgrade keeps, and only then offers «ترقية وربط», sent with `upgradeExisting`. Only an account with exactly this email counts: with none (the search also finds longer emails that hold it), or a failed read, no upgrade is offered, the sheet says to check the email, and nothing can be sent until the email changes. Changing the email goes back to creating.
- **A new account's password** shows once, in the [`users`](users.md#behaviour-and-flows) dialog the page puts in the sheet's slot, after the sheet closes; an upgrade shows none, and its toast names the person and the space.

**Linking and unlinking on the web.** Each owner's row has a menu, «إجراءات: {name}»: «ربط بمساحة», unavailable for a suspended account with «الحساب موقوف — استرجعه أولًا» under it, then «فك الربط من {space}» once per space of theirs.
- **Linking** is one dialog with two entries. From an owner's row, or a person's on the users page, «ربط {name} بمساحة»: one space, searched on the server, each option with its state and owners, the person's own spaces offered as «مربوطة» and their front desks as «مكتب استقبال», neither to be chosen. From an unverified space's editor, «ربط مالك بـ {space}»: one person, owners and users by name or email (active accounts only, no admin), each with their email and what they are («مالك · {spaces}», «مالك», «مستخدم»), and a line to the owners page for a new owner with no account. Before «ربط» it says what follows: an unverified space «تصبح … موثّقة ويعدّلها مالكها، ولا يعدّلها فريق مساحة بعد الآن.», a verified one «يصبح مالكًا آخر لـ …؛ تبقى موثّقة.», and, for a user, «ويصبح حسابه حساب مالك، ويُطلب منه الدخول من جديد.». Nothing chosen sends nothing and points to the field. `RECEPTION_LINK_ACTIVE`, `ACCOUNT_IS_ADMIN`, `ACCOUNT_SUSPENDED`, `ACCOUNT_PASSWORD_PENDING`, an account gone (`invalid_choice`) and a space deleted meanwhile are worded in the dialog, which stays open. Linked, it closes once the admin's views have arrived again, and a toast names the person and the space, adding «المساحة موثّقة الآن» when it was not.
- **Unlinking** asks first, «فك ربط {person} من {space}؟», with the space's owners read as it opens: its last owner «تصبح … غير موثّقة وتختفي حالتها المباشرة، ويعدّلها فريق مساحة.», others «يبقى المالكون الآخرون، وتبقى موثّقة.»; the line and «فك الربط» wait while they are read, and a failed read says «إن كان مالكها الوحيد، تصبح غير موثّقة وتختفي حالتها المباشرة.». It opens on «رجوع»; «فك الربط» is destructive and stays until the answer, so a space deleted meanwhile is said inside. Unlinked, a toast names both, adding «المساحة غير موثّقة الآن» for the last owner. An owner whose last space it was leaves the list, and the focus moves to the list.
- **From a space's editor** (`SpaceOwnersActions`, in its notice through the page's slot): a verified space's notice offers «فك ربط {name}» per owner, the same unlink; an unverified one's warning offers «ربط مالك», the same link from the space's end, which waits while the editor has unsaved changes — marked, still reached by the keyboard, «احفظ التغييرات أو تجاهلها أولًا» beside it and read with it, opening nothing. Linked, the editor turns read-only; the last owner unlinked, it turns editable. Either way, and when one of several owners is unlinked, the button that opened the dialog is gone, and the focus moves to the notice the editor gives.

**The owner's staff on the web** (`features/staff`). «الموظفون» says what a receptionist reaches («يصل موظف الاستقبال إلى المكتب والزبائن والإعلانات ودفعاته اليوم، ولا يرى المالية ولا الإعدادات.»), then the space's reception accounts, every one, in the server's order: from 768 px a table — the name with the email under it, left to right, and the state — and a card per person below it.
- **The state** is badges: «نشط» or «بلا وصول»; while active with a temporary password still to be changed, «لم يغيّر كلمة المرور المؤقتة بعد», or past its expiry, «انتهت صلاحية كلمة المرور المؤقتة».
- **The list** has its own loading, its failure with the request's reference and «إعادة المحاولة», and an empty state, «لا يوجد موظفون», that offers «إضافة موظف»; with people listed, their count («موظف واحد», «3 موظفين»…) and «إضافة موظف» sit above it.
- **«إضافة موظف»** opens a sheet from the end, «الدور: الاستقبال»: the name and the email, then what adding does, said before the submit — «يُنشأ حساب جديد لهذا البريد برمز مرور مؤقت يظهر لك مرة واحدة — جهّز طريقة تسليمه. لا يُربط حساب موجود.» — and «إنشاء حساب الاستقبال». Once the list has arrived again, the sheet closes and the account's temporary password shows once, in `users`' one-time dialog, its handover recorded on this module's staff route; the focus then returns to «إضافة موظف». An email that already has an account is refused on the email, in the owner's words: never linked, another email, or, for someone on the staff list, their row's own way back — their access given back, or a new temporary password; a 403 says only that it is no longer open to the owner, a 404 that the space no longer is, and fetches the owner's own spaces again; too many attempts count down. Any failure of the add reads the list again, so an account the server made while its answer was lost shows with its pending password and its re-issue.
- **Each row's menu**, «إجراءات: {name}», is busy while one of its actions waits — still in the focus order, but it does not open. An active person's: «كلمة مرور مؤقتة جديدة» while a temporary password is pending, else held, «غيّر كلمة المرور المؤقتة؛ إن نسي كلمة مروره يستعيدها بالبريد.» under it; then, after a separator, «إيقاف وصوله». A deactivated person's: «إعادة وصوله», then the re-issue held, «أعد وصوله أولًا.».
- **«إيقاف وصوله»** asks first, «إيقاف وصول {name}؟ — تنتهي كل جلساته على كل الأجهزة فورًا، ولا يصل إلى هذه المساحة حتى تعيد وصوله. يبقى اسمه على الدفعات وفي السجل.», opening on «رجوع»; «إيقاف الوصول» stays until the answer, a refusal inside; done, the answer replaces the row and the focus returns to its menu, with no toast.
- **«إعادة وصوله»** acts at once: the answer replaces the row, with no toast; a refusal is a toast that stays, «تعذّر إعادة وصول {name}», with its reason.
- **«كلمة مرور مؤقتة جديدة»** asks first, «تحلّ محلّ كلمة المرور المؤقتة السابقة وتنتهي جلساته…», «إصدار» staying until the answer; issued, the new password shows once in the same one-time dialog, its handover recorded on the space's route, and the list is read again for its new expiry. A password no longer pending, or a person no longer on the staff (404), also reads the list again.
- **Every refusal is in the owner's words** (`staff.refusals`): a suspended account, an account become an admin's or holding another space, a password already changed; a 404 that the person is no longer on the staff or the space no longer open to them; a 403 only that it is no longer open to the owner. Any 404 on the staff routes, the list's read included, fetches the owner's own spaces again, so the switcher stops offering a space whose link ended (`shared/api`'s `useLinkEnded`, as the packages' routes do).

## Decisions

### API decisions

- **The caller's spaces are open to any signed-in user:** the active links with the role at each, oldest first. *Why:* the path names no space, so no space middleware applies. 2026-10-04, #36.
- **A hidden space is listed; a deleted one is not.** *Why:* an owner still manages a hidden space. 2026-10-04, #36.
- **Names come in both languages, with the area's;** a retired area still names its spaces. 2026-10-04, #36.
- **The list is composed from three modules, one query each.** *Why:* the module levels, and no N+1. 2026-10-04, #36.
- **A link to a deleted space counts for nothing, for access and in the lists,** the session's links included; deleting a space writes nothing in the links. The global role and the admin's deleted spaces are the exceptions (below). *Why:* the delete stays reversible, and a restored space has its links again. It replaced #36's open question, under which the two lists disagreed ([finding 18](../architecture/findings/18-whether-a-link-to-a-deleted-space-still-counts.md)). 2026-10-06, #44.
- **The admin's spaces list is composed here:** the verified and governorate filters resolve to ids first, then `spaces` filters and pages; each row carries its owners, its stale groups and its last update. *Why:* every page full, and the total right. 2026-10-06, #44.
- **The list's states are lowercase, like its filter.** *Why:* one vocabulary. 2026-10-06, #44.
- **The links loader runs without its refusal on the admin's space routes,** and gives no links for a deleted space. *Why:* `can()` reads whether a space is verified there too. 2026-10-06, #44.
- **The owners page is a view of the users list,** `GET /admin/users?role=owner`, with no list of its own (the owner's A6). *Why:* one list, one search and one state filter for every account. 2026-10-11, #60.
- **Adding an owner creates the account, or upgrades it, and links it to one space, in one transaction** (D1). *Why:* all or nothing; more spaces are linked one at a time. 2026-10-11, #60.
- **An email with an account is refused first (`EMAIL_HAS_ACCOUNT`), then upgraded only when asked (`upgradeExisting`),** keeping its name and password (D2). *Why:* the admin is told before another person's account changes (A5). The flag allows an upgrade without requiring one. 2026-10-11, #60.
- **The space chosen in the body is refused on its field** (S4): 422 `invalid_choice`, as a body's `areaId` is. *Why:* the form shows it there; the routes on one space keep their 404. 2026-10-11, #60.
- **The admin links owners one space at a time, owners only;** reception links are the owner's (step 6). *Why:* the owner's D1 and D4: one consequence said before each act. 2026-10-11, #60.
- **Ending a link deactivates it, and linking again reactivates the same row,** for an owner as for reception; a deactivated reception row linked as an owner's becomes one, and its audit entry says the role changed (the owner's addition). *Why:* one rule for every link, and payments and the audit name the person. 2026-10-11, #60.
- **The global `OWNER` follows the active owner links, a soft-deleted space's included:** the first makes an owner, the end of the last a user, each change ending the person's sessions; a link that leaves the role as it is ends none (D7, S6). *Why:* ADR 0009's label kept in step; deleting a space writes nothing in its links, so it changes no role. *So:* a person whose only owner links are to deleted spaces stays `OWNER`, with no space in the users list, and an unlink of a deleted space answers 404: the way back to `USER` is to restore the space, unlink, and delete it again (the owner's option A, [finding 50](../architecture/findings/50-an-owner-whose-only-spaces-are-deleted-stays-an-owner.md)). 2026-10-11, #60.
- **Linking an owner already linked writes nothing, and answers as a success** (S3). *Why:* a link is idempotent by design ([ADR 0015](../architecture/decisions/0015-idempotency-and-concurrency.md)); a retry after a lost answer succeeds. The placeholder code `OWNER_ALREADY_LINKED` went. 2026-10-11, #60.
- **A person with an active reception link at the space is refused, not moved** (D5). *Why:* the reception link is the owner's to end; no silent change of role at a space. 2026-10-11, #60.
- **Every link change takes the space's lock, then the person's** (S5). *Why:* a save on the space decides on the links as they are ([finding 43](../architecture/findings/43-two-races-around-a-spaces-facts.md)), and two ends of one person's links at once each count what the other left. 2026-10-11, #60.
- **The users list is composed here, `users` filtering and paging its own accounts first,** newest first; each row adds how it signs in and when it joined (the owner's S2). *Why:* every filter is the account's own, so the page is cut before the links are read; the users screen shows both columns, so the users item adds actions only. 2026-10-11, #60.
- **The admin's role change is composed here,** because only the links tell whether a role may change; `users` writes it (D1, D3). It offers `USER` or `ADMIN`, and refuses a person with any active link, owner or reception, a soft-deleted space's included, with `ACCOUNT_HAS_LINKS`. *Why:* an admin is never linked (`ACCOUNT_IS_ADMIN`), the `OWNER` follows the links ([ADR 0009](../architecture/decisions/0009-space-scoped-reception-role.md)), and no change happens silently at a space. 2026-10-11, the users item.
- **A role change takes the person's lock with every active admin's row, and counts the links under it;** it takes no space's lock. *Why:* every link change takes the person's lock, so a link made meanwhile counts, and two admins demoting each other at once cannot leave none ([conventions › Concurrency](../backend/conventions.md#concurrency)). 2026-10-11, the users item.
- **A row carries its state as facts apart, `isVerified`, `isHidden` and `deletedAt`,** as `AdminSpace` does; the lowercase vocabulary is the status filter's alone (`SPACE_STATUSES`). *Why:* a state is two facts (the owner's A3): a hidden space keeps its verification, which the screen shows beside it. It replaced the row's one `state`, hidden first. 2026-10-12, the deleted-spaces item.
- **`status=deleted` lists the soft-deleted spaces alone,** hidden or not, verified or not, with the search, the place, stale or missing, the order and the pages of the others; every other status, and none, leaves them out (the owner's A3). *Why:* a space deleted by mistake can be restored once its toast has gone ([finding 44](../architecture/findings/44-no-screen-restores-a-deleted-space-once-its-toast-has-gone.md)); one list, filtered before the page is cut. 2026-10-12, the deleted-spaces item.
- **A deleted space's row names its owners, and is verified by them,** the second exception to "a link to a deleted space counts for nothing". *Why:* its links are kept as they are, and the way out of [finding 50](../architecture/findings/50-an-owner-whose-only-spaces-are-deleted-stays-an-owner.md), restore then unlink, needs to know whose space it was; the row says what a restore brings back. 2026-10-12, the deleted-spaces item.
- **The row dates a deletion (`deletedAt`), `null` for a live space** (the owner's choice over a flag). *Why:* the screen shows a deleted row as deleted on that day. 2026-10-12, the deleted-spaces item.
- **The admin-spaces composition supplies active owner names to the single-space admin read** (`ownersFor`), while its list composition remains unchanged. *Why:* `spaces` is an L1 capability and must not import `space-links` (L2); the composition root injects the reader so the read and writes carry owner names under the existing space share lock. 2026-10-15, #82.
- **The owner creates new reception accounts only,** inserted without a lookup, so the users' unique key refuses an email that has an account (`EMAIL_HAS_ACCOUNT`) and no existing account is ever linked by an owner (ADR 0009, revised). *Why:* the email is typed by the owner and may be wrong: linking would hand a stranger's account the desk, or the desk to a stranger ([finding 84](../architecture/findings/84-a-person-cannot-work-at-two-spaces-front-desks.md)). 2026-10-18, #90.
- **The staff list is answered whole,** every reception link, deactivated ones included, oldest first, each with its pending temporary password's expiry. *Why:* a space has few staff, and the owner's page shows each one's state, an expired password included. 2026-10-18, #90.
- **The staff live in `space-links`,** in `staff/`, its routes in `space-links.manage.routes.ts`, every account write a `users` function. *Why:* the links are this module's table; the account is `users`' ([ADR 0013](../architecture/decisions/0013-identity-modules.md)). 2026-10-18, #90.
- **A deactivation or a re-activation takes the space's lock, then the person's, and acts on a reception link only,** read by its role, so a co-owner's link is never touched (404); the deactivation ends every session of the person, and a re-activation is refused for a suspended account or an admin; the answer is the member as it now is. *Why:* the desk's device must not stay signed in; an admin or a suspended account must not regain a desk through it (P6); the screen updates its row without another read (P7). 2026-10-18, #90.
- **The owner re-issues a temporary password only while one is pending, whoever issued it (the owner's own, or an admin's recovery), and only to an account that holds nothing else** (a `USER` with no other active link, S5); the handover is recorded only for an active reception link here, while a temporary password is pending. *Why:* a lost or expired answer is recovered as the admin's is ([finding 49](../architecture/findings/49-a-lost-answer-to-adding-an-owner-loses-its-temporary-password.md)); an account that holds anything else must not be taken by this space's owner, and the admin gives a pending account no new power (S6). 2026-10-18, #90.
- **The admin links, upgrades or makes an admin no account whose temporary password is pending** (`ACCOUNT_PASSWORD_PENDING`, S6), under the account's lock, after the other refusals; the admin's own new owners are created, not linked, so they are not concerned. *Why:* the owner who created a receptionist holds their temporary password: an upgrade to another space's owner, or the admin's role, would be the owner's too ([security › Passwords](../backend/security.md#passwords)). 2026-10-18, #90.
- **A users list row carries the person's reception links (`receptionSpaces`) beside the spaces they own,** read in the same query as the owner links and split by role, a soft-deleted space left out. *Why:* the admin sees who works at which desk (DI-24); the field is additive. 2026-10-18, #90.
- **Staff accounts count under their own limit, 10 an hour per owner,** after `requireCan` and before the body is read, the creation and the re-issue alike (S3). *Why:* each costs a bcrypt hash, a creation an account row, and a re-issue ends a person's sessions ([security › Rate limits](../backend/security.md#rate-limits-fixed-window)). 2026-10-18, #90.

### Web decisions

- **The web reads under `['me', 'spaces']`.** *Why:* the user's own data, in its key scope. 2026-10-04, #36.
- **`space-links` is dashboard-only** on the web. 2026-10-05, #39.
- **The switcher lives in this capability:** name and area, the other spaces, navigation on a choice, a skeleton, a retry. 2026-10-05, #39.
- **An English-only name shows marked `lang="en"` in the Arabic interface.** *Why:* the space name's language rule ([localisation › Content in two languages](../frontend/localisation.md#content-in-two-languages)). 2026-10-06, #44.
- **The admin's list on the web is one section with two slots, filled by the page:** each row's actions and the place field. *Why:* the page mixes three capabilities, so it composes them, and no feature imports another ([architecture §3](../frontend/architecture.md#what-a-feature-exports)); the edit screen and the owner's screens can reuse the seam. 2026-10-07, #50.
- **Each space's name is a link to the path the page gives** (`spaceLink`), the space's editor on the admin's page. *Why:* a row's name is what the admin looks for; the feature knows no routes. 2026-10-13, #74.
- **The list's filters and page live in the address,** each read by the contract's own rule, an invalid one falling back to its default; a filter replaces the address and returns to the first page, the pages are links. *Why:* a reload, a link or Back shows the same list, and Back leaves the page rather than stepping through filters, as the lookups' tab does. 2026-10-07, #50.
- **The list's page size is the contract's 20,** and the web does not send it. 2026-10-07, #50.
- **Freshness shows what is stale on a warning badge and what is missing on a warning badge each,** both when both apply, else "Up to date". *Why:* both need the admin's attention (the owner's A8). It replaced #50's missing on a neutral badge, a missing group being as wanted as a stale one. 2026-10-15, #84.
- **"Last update" is the day and the month, and the year when it is not the current one,** on Gaza's calendar, formatted inside the feature. *Why:* the design's short date, without a date from last year passing for this one; a platform formatter waits for a second feature that formats dates. 2026-10-07, #50.
- **While another page or filter loads, the page shown stays, marked busy** (#50's review). *Why:* a page link chosen from the keyboard kept no place while its page loaded, and the focus fell back to the list. 2026-10-08, #50.
- **The search field keeps what was typed;** it changes only when the address reads another search. *Why:* the address's search is trimmed, so a field reset to it after a pause ate the space before the next word (#50's review). 2026-10-08, #50.
- **When the row that held the focus leaves, the focus moves to the list.** *Why:* a deleted space's row takes its menu with it, and the focus would otherwise fall to the page's start. 2026-10-07, #50.
- **The admin's list reads under `['admin', 'space-links', 'spaces', request]`,** and the admin's writes on a space refresh the admin scope whole ([spaces › Decisions](spaces.md#decisions)). *Why:* the admin's scope, and no feature names another's keys. 2026-10-07, #50.
- **The users list lives in `space-links`, beside the spaces list,** with its read, its row, its search and its state filter. *Why:* a web capability carries the name of the backend module it calls, and `GET /admin/users` is this module's composed read, as the spaces list is; the paged list's pieces are reused, not copied; the users page will fill its row actions through a slot, as `spaces` fills the spaces list's. 2026-10-18, #88.
- **The owners page is the users list with `role=owner` fixed by its own hook;** the search and the state are the users list's (`?q=&state=&page=`). *Why:* the users page reuses the read, the row, the filters' address and the query. 2026-10-18, #88.
- **The owners page's state filter is chips, «الكل · نشط · موقوف», at every width, with no sheet.** *Why:* a single status filter of four or fewer choices is a `ToggleGroup` (P5), and a filter is a chip (A7); the design's sheet held that one filter only. 2026-10-18, #88.
- **An owner with no space reads that every space is deleted, the whole line a link to the deleted spaces.** *Why:* the list leaves a deleted space out while the role still counts its link, so the row would otherwise show an owner of nothing; the restore is where the way out begins ([finding 50](../architecture/findings/50-an-owner-whose-only-spaces-are-deleted-stays-an-owner.md)). 2026-10-18, #88.
- **The list's pages, states, paused search, return to the last page and focus keeper are pieces of the capability,** shared by the spaces and owners lists. *Why:* the owners list is their second instance (engineering principles §1). 2026-10-18, #88.
- **The users list's core — its read, states, count, pages, search and state chips — is shared by the owners and the users pages** (`useAdminUsersList`), each with its own words; the toolbar is one, its role chips offered when the role is not fixed. *Why:* the users page is its second instance (engineering principles §1); a copy would drift. 2026-10-19, #93.
- **The owners page never reads a role from its address.** *Why:* its role is fixed; `?role=admin` would otherwise count as a filter, «لا يوجد … يطابقون البحث», while still asking for owners. 2026-10-19, #93.
- **The users page's role and state filters are chips, «الكل · مستخدم · مالك · أدمن» and «الكل · نشط · موقوف», at every width, with no sheet.** *Why:* a status filter of four choices or fewer is a `ToggleGroup` (P5), and a filter is a chip (A7); the design's two `Select`s in a sheet held those two filters only (design issue 1). 2026-10-19, #93.
- **The users list is cards below 1024 px** (`DataTable cardsBelow="lg"`). *Why:* A7 draws it as cards until 1024; the design copied the table's card markup (DI-23). 2026-10-19, #93.
- **The users page's row menu is composed by the page,** this capability exporting the link's item, its dialog's state and its dialog (`useUserLinkActions`, `useUserLinkDialog`, `UserLinkDialog`), rather than a menu of its own taking another capability's items through a slot. *Why:* a component that combines several capabilities lives in neither ([architecture › What a feature exports](../frontend/architecture.md#what-a-feature-exports)). 2026-10-19, #93.
- **The dialog a row's menu opens is held by the page, above the list,** not in the row (#93's review, the owner's decision; it amends the plan's "beside the menu"). Closed, the focus returns to the person's menu button as it is then. *Why:* a row is drawn again when the admin's views arrive after an action, and a card becomes a table row when the width crosses 1024 px; a dialog held in the row closed with it, and the one-time password with it. 2026-10-20, #93.
- **The signed-in admin's own row reads «أنت» in place of its actions.** *Why:* the server refuses every action on one's own account (`OWN_ACCOUNT`). 2026-10-19, #93.
- **The role change is held up front, with its reason, for every case the row can see** — an owner (leading to their links), an owner of deleted spaces (leading to them), a receptionist — **and the server's refusal says the rest** (a deleted space's link, a link made meanwhile), without guessing at a desk. *Why:* a disabled action says why (foundation §10); the row carries the desks now (DI-24), so the design's «استقبال على الأرجح» guess is gone (design issue 3). 2026-10-19, #93.
- **The role change's action is a destructive button that stays until the answer,** not a closing `AlertDialogAction`, its dialog opening on «رجوع». *Why:* a refusal is worded in the dialog, which a closing action would lose (P8, design issue 2). 2026-10-19, #93.
- **The role change says so in a toast, though the row then shows the new role.** *Why:* P8's exception is for in-row controls; this one acts through a dialog, and the row may leave a list filtered by role. 2026-10-19, #93.
- **A person's front desks are offered in the link's spaces as «مكتب استقبال», not to be chosen,** as their own spaces are «مربوطة». *Why:* the server refuses that link (`RECEPTION_LINK_ACTIVE`), and the row now knows the desks (DI-24), so the admin is told before choosing. 2026-10-19, #93.
- **Adding an owner asks for the upgrade only after `EMAIL_HAS_ACCOUNT`, and offers it only once the email's account is shown and matches it exactly** (the owner's security line, 2026-10-18). *Why:* the upgrade changes someone else's account; a mistyped email that belongs to another person must never become an owner by one click, so the admin sees whose account it is first. The account is the users list's row (no API change), its search being by part of a name or an email, so the web keeps the exact match only. 2026-10-18, #89.
- **The upgrade confirm lists the account's reception desks** ([finding 88](../architecture/findings/88-the-upgrade-confirm-shows-no-front-desks.md)). *Why:* an account the server lets be upgraded owns no space, so its desks are what tells the admin whose it is. 2026-10-19, #93.
- **One link dialog, two entries** (A4): from a space's editor, one person to choose (owners and users, by name or email, `state=active`, an admin left out, each saying what it is); from an owner's row, one space (its state and owners, the person's own spaces offered but not to be chosen, «مربوطة»). *Why:* one way to link, whichever end the admin starts from. 2026-10-19, #91.
- **The link says its consequence before the act, from the chosen option's own facts,** as the latest search answer has them (from a space, the editor's read: [finding 95](../architecture/findings/95-a-link-from-the-editor-words-the-space-from-the-editors-read.md)); the role line («ويصبح حسابه حساب مالك…») only for a chosen `USER`, when it is certain. *Why:* the admin sees what changes before it does; a role said on a guess would be false for an owner. 2026-10-19, #91.
- **The unlink reads the space's owners each time it opens** (`GET /admin/spaces/:spaceId`), its line waiting, and its action with it, until they come — an earlier opening's answer in the cache never stands in for this one's; a failed read says the line true either way. *Why:* an owner's row cannot tell whether they are the space's last owner, and the editor's copy may be stale; a destructive consequence is said from the server's answer (the plan's Stronger 2). 2026-10-19, #91. Once the unlink is sent, the line stays as it was read while the refresh that follows reads the space again, rather than falling back to its placeholder (#91's executor's note, fixed in #93). 2026-10-20, #93.
- **The unlink never predicts the person's role.** *Why:* a deleted space's link still counts for the role while no list shows it ([finding 50](../architecture/findings/50-an-owner-whose-only-spaces-are-deleted-stays-an-owner.md)), so «لا يبقى مالكًا» could be false. 2026-10-19, #91.
- **A link or an unlink refreshes the admin scope whole, the space's editor included,** and stays pending until it has arrived. *Why:* either changes the space's verification, and so what its editor allows. 2026-10-19, #91.
- **While the editor has unsaved changes, «ربط مالك» waits, marked, not disabled, saying «احفظ التغييرات أو تجاهلها أولًا»** (design issue 7). *Why:* a link turns the editor read-only, which would drop them unasked; a disabled button leaves the tab order, and the keyboard would never meet it or its reason, as the spaces list's row actions wait marked ([spaces › Decisions](spaces.md#decisions)). 2026-10-19, #91.
- **After a link or an unlink from the editor, the focus returns to the button that opened the dialog, or, when it is gone, to the notice.** *Why:* a link or the last unlink swaps the notice's content whole, and an unlinked owner's button leaves; a focus left on a removed button falls to the page's start (#91's review). 2026-10-19, #91.
- **The link's and the unlink's refusals are worded in their dialog, which stays open; the unlink's action is a destructive button that stays until the answer,** not a closing `AlertDialogAction` (P8, design issue 10). *Why:* the admin reads why and chooses again; a dialog closed on a failure loses it. `ACCOUNT_PASSWORD_PENDING` (#90) is worded with the others. 2026-10-19, #91.
- **The add sheet's refusals are worded on the form, which stays open, and its space is searched on the server.** *Why:* the admin corrects the email or the space in place; the spaces are paged, so the `Combobox` takes the caller's search (O1). 2026-10-18, #89.
- **The one-time password dialog comes through a slot the owners page fills, rendered beside the sheet, not in it,** and the add's answer is not kept: the mutation is reset once the password is handed on. *Why:* the dialog is `users`' and no feature imports another; the sheet closes once the owner is added; no password stays in the cache. 2026-10-18, #89. The slot also hands the page «إضافة مالك», where the dialog returns the focus once done (#93's review). 2026-10-20, #93.
- **A space offered in a dialog is worded by the spaces list's row rules** — its name, its state's badges, «مخفية» on the list's own warning, its owners isolated — and the space search is named by its own words, «بحث» and «اسم المساحة», apart from the field it fills. *Why:* one space must not carry two looks (A3: the row view decides the set); the choices were the rules' third instance (engineering principles §1); a screen reader met three controls named «اختر المساحة» (#89's review). 2026-10-19, #91.
- *Superseded 2026-10-15 by #84's decisions, below.* **The row's badge stays one, hidden first, else verified or unverified,** read from the row's facts. *Why:* the list looks as it did until A3's screen shows the two facts apart. 2026-10-12, the deleted-spaces item.
- *Superseded 2026-10-15 by #84's decisions, below.* **The list keeps its own statuses, the contract's but `deleted`,** read from the address and offered in the filter; `?status=deleted` falls back to all. *Why:* the deleted spaces' screen (A3) is not built, and their rows would offer Hide and Delete. 2026-10-12, the deleted-spaces item.
- **A live row's state is badges, one per fact:** the verification always, then «مخفية» when hidden; the row view decides the set, so the table and the card cannot disagree. *Why:* A3 — a state is two facts, and hiding a space no longer erases who edits it. It replaced #50's one badge, hidden first. 2026-10-15, #84.
- **The list offers the contract's four statuses, «محذوفة» among them,** each read from the address by the contract's own rule, so `?status=deleted` reaches the server and survives the filters' and pages' links. *Why:* the API already lists them alone ([finding 44](../architecture/findings/44-no-screen-restores-a-deleted-space-once-its-toast-has-gone.md)); the web needed no status of its own. 2026-10-15, #84.
- **A deleted row is muted, its name text that leads nowhere,** and its date is worded by the list's own short date. *Why:* a deleted space's editor answers 404, and a link there would only say so. 2026-10-15, #84.
- **A deleted row carries one muted line and no badge, as the design draws it:** «محذوفة · …» where its state was, a dash where its freshness was, and nothing else. *Why:* a space that no longer exists cannot be verified or missing facts; its verification badge and its freshness warnings would be read on a row whose owner is restoring it, and the design chose the line (#84's review, the owner's decision). 2026-10-17, #85.
- **The spaces list keeps a card per space until 1024 px** (`DataTable cardsBelow="lg"`), its filters inline, not in a sheet, from 768 px. *Why:* A7 draws the spaces, users and data-report lists as cards until 1024; the spaces list's seven columns were cut at 768. 2026-10-19, #93.
- **The stale filter is a `ToggleGroup` chip by the count, not a field** (A7), and the count moved beside the search to stand with it; the phone's sheet keeps the two fields only, and counts those two only, leaving the chip alone when it clears them. *Why:* a switch is not a filter field, and the count is what the admin looks for after filtering (the design's P5); a sheet that counted and cleared a field it does not hold would read a chip the sheet never showed (#84's review). 2026-10-15, #84.
- **The owner's staff are a web feature of their own, `staff`, over this module, and documented here** (S5). *Why:* a feature finer than its module serves a different audience on different screens ([architecture §3](../frontend/architecture.md#3-capabilities-features)), and a web feature over the same module is a section of that module's document ([documentation §5](../architecture/documentation.md#5-capability-documents)). 2026-10-20, #99.
- **A staff row is the name with the email under it, and the state; no joining date and no payments** (design issue 9). *Why:* `StaffMember` carries no date, and a receptionist's payments are the payments step's. 2026-10-20, #99.
- **The add sheet says a new account is created and none linked, before its submit** (design issue 10). *Why:* since ADR 0009's revision an existing account is never linked, so the design's line was false; someone already on the staff comes back, or gets a new password, through their row; and a failed add reads the list again, since a lost answer may hide an account made (#99's review). 2026-10-20, #99.
- **The staff screen words its refusals itself, in `staff`'s copy; the shared errors catalogue keeps the admin's** (finding 90). *Why:* the codes were first written for the admin, whose words would send an owner where they cannot go. 2026-10-20, #99.
- **A held staff action stays in the menu with its reason under it** (S10, design issue 11): «كلمة مرور مؤقتة جديدة» while nothing is pending, and on a deactivated row. *Why:* the owner learns the way out instead of meeting a missing item. 2026-10-20, #99.
- **No toast for a removed or given-back access; a failure's toast stays** (design issue 12). *Why:* the row shows it (P8). 2026-10-20, #99.
- **The deactivation and the re-issue ask in a dialog whose action stays until the answer, a refusal inside,** and the list holds the dialogs above its rows. *Why:* as the admin's suspension: a refusal must be read where it was asked, and a row drawn again must never close one. 2026-10-20, #99.
- **The one-time password lives in the staff page's state alone,** the add's answer reset once handed on (`gcTime: 0`). *Why:* as the admin's ([finding 34](../architecture/findings/34-passwords-stay-in-the-mutation-cache-after-a-sign-in-a-registration-or-a-password-change.md)). 2026-10-20, #99.
