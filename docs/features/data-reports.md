# Data reports

> **Status:** Active · **Class:** Description — what is built, as the code shows it · **Last Updated:** 2026-10-29 · **Owner:** Basel Ghoneim
> **Authority:** The `data-reports` capability: a signed-in user tells Masaha that one field group of a listed space's page is wrong. Its endpoints are owned by the [API contract](../api/api-contract.md#data-reports); its entity by the [data model](../architecture/data-model.md#operations). The API is built; on the web, the owner's and the admin's «بلاغات البيانات», a signed-in user's «بلاغاتي» and the report from a space's page.
> **Scope:** the API module `data-reports` (L3); `@masaha/shared/data-reports`; the web feature `features/data-reports`, any page group: the owner's list of a space's reports, the admin's list of every space's, and the decision sheet; the user's own reports, and the report's button and dialog a space's page sets.

## What it does

- Lets a signed-in user report a listed space's field group — prices, hours, contact, location, amenities, or «أخرى» with a message — at most 10 reports an hour.
- Answers a repeat while the user's report of that space and field is open with that report, writing nothing.
- Pages the user's own reports, newest first, open or decided, each with its decision and who replied, and whether its space is still listed.
- Pages a space's reports for its owner, by status and field, never showing who reported; lets the owner resolve one, with a reply or none, or dismiss it with a reply. A decided report is final; the same decision again answers it as it is.
- Pages every report for the admin, by status, field and space, with who reported and who decided and whether the space is verified; lets the admin decide an unverified space's.

## Who uses it

- **A signed-in user,** through the API: `POST /spaces/:slug/reports`, and their own, `GET /me/reports`. On the site, the user reads «بلاغاتي» (`/me/reports`) and the replies, linked from the account menu, and reports from a space's page; a guest's press asks them to sign in first (2026-10-28, #129).
- **The space's owner,** under `/manage/spaces/:spaceId/data-reports`: the list and the decision. Reception is refused. On the dashboard, the owner reads «بلاغات البيانات» (`/dashboard/spaces/:spaceId/reports`) and decides each report in its sheet (2026-10-28, #129).
- **The admin,** under `/admin/data-reports`: every report, and the decision of an unverified space's. On the dashboard, the admin reads «بلاغات البيانات» (`/dashboard/admin/reports`), filters it by one space with [space-links](space-links.md)' search, and decides an unverified space's report in the same sheet (2026-10-28, #129).
- **The overviews** (`overview`): the owner's counts the space's open reports, the admin's the platform's, by who handles them (2026-10-29, #131).

## Responsibility boundary

- **Owns** the `data_reports` table, a report's rules (one open per user, space and field; a decision's fields together; a decided report final) and the report limit.
- **Leaves** who may read and decide to `can()` (`space.dataReports.read`, `dataReports.resolve`, [security › Authorization](../backend/security.md#authorization)), on the links [`space-links`](space-links.md) loads; the space's lock to `spaces`.
- **Leaves** whether a space is publicly listed to [`spaces`](spaces.md) (`publicSpace.findShown`) and [`lookups`](lookups.md) (`isActiveArea`), as the space's page asks them; a space's names to `spaces` (`namesWithDeleted`), whether a space is verified to `space-links` (`verifiedSpaceIds`, `linksAt` under the lock), the people's names to `users` (`namesFor`), and which spaces are listed to its public listing (`publicListing.upTo`, with the areas [`lookups`](lookups.md) lists); the user's lock to [`users`](users.md) (`lockAccount`).

## How it composes the platform

- Its me router sits at the API's root (`/spaces/:slug/reports`, `/me/reports`), beside the directory's public routes; the route asks `requireAuth`, then counts the request by the user (`DATA_REPORT`, [security › Rate limits](../backend/security.md#rate-limits-fixed-window)), then checks the body by the shared schema ([shared-package.md](../architecture/shared-package.md)).
- Its manage router sits at `/data-reports` below `/manage/spaces/:spaceId`, behind the one boundary ([conventions › Routers](../backend/conventions.md#routers)); each route asks `can()` first through `requireCan`, and the decision again under the space's lock ([conventions › Space access](../backend/conventions.md#space-access)).
- Its admin router sits at `/data-reports` inside `/admin`, behind `requireRole('ADMIN')`; its decision asks `can()` on the links `space-links` reads under the space's lock.
- A decision is audited in its own transaction ([conventions §6](../backend/conventions.md#6-audit)).
- A report and a decision are dated by the one clock ([conventions §11](../backend/conventions.md#11-time)).

## Behaviour and flows

**Report.** The space as its page finds it — shown (not hidden, not deleted) and in an active area — else `not_found`; its names. Then, in one transaction: the user's row locked (`users.lockAccount`, the session lock), so one user's reports are serialised; the user's open report of that space and field, answered as it is, 200, writing nothing; else the new report, open, 201. Two identical reports at once: the second waits on the lock and finds the first; the partial unique index is the backstop. A report is not audited (the audit lists decisions, [conventions §6](../backend/conventions.md#6-audit)).

**The user's reports.** A page of the user's rows, newest first, then id, filtered open or decided, and its count; then, for the page's spaces, their names, soft-deleted ones included, and which of them the public listing lists now — one query each. `repliedBy` is read from who decided, as stored with the decision (`OWNER` → `SPACE`, `ADMIN` → `MASAHA`).

**The owner's list.** A page of the space's rows, newest first, by status and field, and its count. The repository's select names no reporter column, so no mapper can show one.

**The admin's list.** A page of every report whose space is not deleted, newest first, by status, field, space and who handles it (`handledBy`: the verified spaces' ids read first, only for that filter), with who reported and who decided; then the spaces' names, the verified spaces' ids and the people's names, one query each.

**Decide.** The report's space, read without a lock, must be the path's (else `not_found`). Then, in one transaction: the space's lock; `can('dataReports.resolve')` on the links read under it, so a link ended meanwhile decides; the report's row, locked (`FOR UPDATE`); `decisionOf` (pure): an open report is decided; one decided with the same status, reply and decider is answered as it is, writing nothing; any other decision is `REPORT_ALREADY_DECIDED`. The decision writes its status, who, when, the reply and the side (`OWNER`) together, audited. The admin's decision is the same, with any space's report, the links read through `space-links` under the lock (so the admin decides an unverified space's only, and an owner linked meanwhile makes it `forbidden`), and the side `ADMIN`. The owner and the admin at once: both wait on the space's lock, and the links are read under it before the report is. An owner's decision while the admin's waits makes the admin's `forbidden` (403) while the owner is still linked, and `REPORT_ALREADY_DECIDED` once the space is unverified again; the admin's decision first makes the owner's `REPORT_ALREADY_DECIDED`. Lock order: the space's row, then the report's; a report's create takes the user's row only, so no two orders cross.

**The owner's reports on the web** (`features/data-reports`, composed by `pages/dashboard/space/reports/DataReportsPage.tsx`). «بلاغات البيانات» says what it lists, then «الكل · جديدة · تمت المراجعة» — the server filters `OPEN` or `DECIDED`, so «تمت المراجعة» is either decision — the chip and the page in the address (`?status=open|decided&page=`), and the count. It lists `GET …/data-reports` 20 a page, newest first: from 768 px a table of five columns — the field, the report on two lines at most in its own direction («لم يكتب المُبلِّغ تفاصيل.», muted, when there is none), the day on Gaza's calendar and never the time («21 أكتوبر», the year when it is not this one), the status («جديد» `info`, «تم التصحيح» `success`, «لم يُعتمد» `neutral`) and «فتح» — and below it a card each, «زائر · {day}» on it. The reporter is never named. Its states: loading, a failure with its reference and a retry, a failed refresh over the rows shown, «لا توجد بلاغات», and a chip that matches nothing, with «عرض الكل». «فتح» opens the decision sheet at the end side, full width below 768 px: «بلاغ عن {field}», «زائر · {day}» and the status; the report in a box; «تعديل الحقل», to Packages & prices for the prices and to the field's section of the space profile for the others («أخرى» to the profile); then, while the report is open, «ردّ إدارة المساحة» — its helper says the reply is shown to the reporter, required for «لم يُعتمد» and optional for «تم التصحيح» — focused first, and «تم التصحيح» and «لم يُعتمد». A blank reply with «لم يُعتمد» sends nothing: the reply is focused with «اكتب السبب؛ هو ما سيقرؤه صاحب البلاغ.». The decision is sent once, with no key; no answer says «لم يصل الرد. أعد المحاولة — القرار نفسه لا يُسجَّل مرتين.» with a retry; `REPORT_ALREADY_DECIDED` and a report gone are worded, the list read again and the sheet read-only; a 403 says «لم يعد هذا متاحًا لك في هذه المساحة.» and the user's spaces are read again. Done, the sheet closes, the space's reports are read again, and the toast says «عُلّم «تم التصحيح»» or «عُلّم «لم يُعتمد»», «في «تمت المراجعة»» with «عرض», and «أُرسل الردّ لصاحب البلاغ.» when a reply was written. A decided report opens read-only: its decision with «الردّ: …» or «بلا ردّ», no buttons. Closed, the sheet gives the focus back to its «فتح», or, that row gone, to the count.

**The admin's reports on the web** (`features/data-reports`, composed by `pages/dashboard/admin/DataReportsPage.tsx`). «بلاغات البيانات» says the admin decides unverified spaces' reports and reads verified spaces' for reference, then the same chips, the space — [space-links](space-links.md)' `AdminSpaceFilter`, which the page sets: a search of the platform's spaces on the server, «كل المساحات» first, which clears it — and the field, every filter and the page in the address (`?status=&field=&space=&page=`), inline from 768 px and in a bottom sheet below it whose button counts what applies. The space and the field in force show under them at every width as removable tags («المساحة: {name}», «الحقل: {field}», each «إزالة فلتر …», [finding 8](../architecture/findings/8-the-stress-tests-applied-filter-tag-has-no-component.md)) beside «مسح الفلاتر», which takes both off; a space no report on the page names says «مساحة محددة». It lists `GET /admin/data-reports` 20 a page: until 1024 px a card each, from it a table of eight columns — the space, a link to its editor; the field; the report; the reporter, by name; the day; the status; «يعالجه» («الإدارة» `info` while the space is unverified, «المالك» `neutral` once verified); «فتح», or «عرض» for a verified space's. Its states as the owner's; a filter that matches nothing offers «عرض الكل», which takes every filter off. The sheet names the reporter («{name} · {day}») and its reply is «ردّ فريقنا»; «تعديل الحقل» leads to the field's section of the space's editor. A verified space's report opens read-only: «مساحة موثّقة — يقرّر مالكها هذا البلاغ. للاطلاع فقط.» with «عرض المساحة», no reply and no buttons. A 403 — the space verified meanwhile — says «هذه المساحة موثّقة: يقرّر مالكها هذا البلاغ.», the admin's reports are read again and the sheet is read-only.

**The user's reports on the web** (`features/data-reports`, composed by `pages/site/account/MyReportsPage.tsx` in the account's layout, signed-in only). «بلاغاتي» says what it lists, then «الكل · قيد المراجعة · تمت المراجعة» — the owner's list's rule: the chip and the page in the address (`?status=open|decided&page=`), a chip back to the first page — and the count. It lists `GET /me/reports` 20 a page, newest first, a card each: the space as its heading, a link to its page while it is listed, its name as muted text once it left with «لم تعد هذه المساحة معروضة في الدليل»; «{field} · أُرسل {day}», the day on Gaza's calendar; the status as the reporter reads it («قيد المراجعة» `info`, «تم التصحيح» `success`, «لم يُعتمد» `neutral`); the message in its own direction, a message over 160 characters folded to two lines with «عرض المزيد» / «عرض أقل»; and the reply, in its own direction, labelled «ردّ إدارة المساحة» or «ردّ فريقنا» by who replied. Its states: three skeleton cards while loading, a failure with its reference and a retry, a failed refresh over the cards shown, «لم ترسل أي بلاغ بعد» with «تصفّح المساحات», and «لا توجد بلاغات هنا» under a chip.

**The report on the web** (`ReportAction`, set by `pages/site/public/space/SpacePage.tsx` in the directory's `report` slot, at the end of the page's main column). «الإبلاغ عن معلومة غير صحيحة» opens a dialog: who reads it, by the space's verification — «بلاغك عن {name} يصل إلى إدارة المساحة، وتجد ردّها في «بلاغاتي».» for a verified space, «…يراجعه فريقنا، وتجد الردّ في «بلاغاتي».» otherwise; «ما المعلومة غير الصحيحة؟», focused first, with the six field groups; «التفاصيل», said optional except for «أخرى», whose missing details say «اكتب ما الخطأ في «أخرى».»; «رجوع» and «إرسال». It is sent once, with no key. A refusal keeps it open with every value, the failure an alert above the footer: a space gone says «لم تعد هذه المساحة في الدليل.», a 429 counts down with the send blocked, and no answer says «لم يصل الرد. أعد المحاولة — لن يُرسَل البلاغ مرتين.» with a retry. Done, it closes, the user's reports are read again, and the toast says where the reply lands — «أرسلنا بلاغك إلى إدارة المساحة. ستجد ردّها في «بلاغاتي».» or «…إلى فريقنا. ستجد الردّ في «بلاغاتي».» — or, when the server answered with the open report (200), «أرسلت بلاغًا عن هذا من قبل، وما زال قيد المراجعة.», each with «عرض بلاغاتي». Closed, the focus returns to the button. A guest's press holds the report in memory and the page asks them to sign in, «سجّل الدخول للإبلاغ عن معلومة», with «إنشاء حساب» and «تسجيل الدخول», each carrying the page as the one to return to; back on the page signed in, the dialog is open. Closing it, or closing the sign-in, lets the held report go.

**The open count** (`openCountOf`, for the owner's overview): how many of the space's reports are `OPEN`, on the owner's list's own status condition, in one `count`: its `status=OPEN` total (2026-10-29, #131).

**The open counts** (`openCounts`, for the admin's overview): the open reports of the live spaces, in all, those the admin handles (an unverified space's) and those the owners handle (a verified one's), by the verified spaces' ids from [`space-links`](space-links.md) and one condition (`handledByWhere`): three counts in parallel (2026-10-29, #131).

## Decisions

### API decisions

- **A repeated report answers the open one, 200, and counts toward the hourly limit** (decision 9). *Why:* a user who taps twice, or retries, must not file two reports the owner reads twice; the limit is counted at the route before the body is read, as every limit of its kind ([conventions §2](../backend/conventions.md#2-layers)). 2026-10-25, #120.
- **The database holds one open report per user, space and field, and a decision's fields together** (S6): a partial unique index and `CHECK`s on the decision's time, decider and side, a dismissal's reply and «أخرى»'s message. *Why:* the race's backstop, and rules that hold for any writer, not only the input schema. 2026-10-25, #120.
- **`repliedBy` is stored with the decision** (`resolved_as`, S7). *Why:* the label follows who answered; derived from the space's verification or the decider's role now, it would change after the fact. 2026-10-25, #120.
- **A space that left the directory stays in the user's list, `listed: false`, with its name** (C7). *Why:* the reply is still the user's to read; the screen shows the name as text, with no link. 2026-10-25, #120.
- **Dismissing needs a reply; resolving takes one or none** (decision 10). *Why:* a reporter told "not changed" deserves the reason; a fixed field speaks for itself. The schema's refinement and a `CHECK` hold it. 2026-10-25, #120.
- **A decided report is final: `REPORT_ALREADY_DECIDED`** (decision 11); **the same decision again answers it as it is** (S8). *Why:* a reporter must not see the reply change; updates are idempotent by design, so a retry after a lost answer is no refusal ([conventions §13](../backend/conventions.md#13-idempotency-and-concurrency)). 2026-10-25, #120.
- **The owner never sees who reported; the admin does** (O12). *Why:* a reporter must not fear the space; the admin, who answers for the platform, sees them. The repository's select leaves the reporter out, so no mapper can leak them. 2026-10-25, #120.
- **The admin decides an unverified space's reports only, read under the space's lock** (`can()` with "verified", [conventions § `data-reports`](../backend/conventions.md#data-reports)). *Why:* once an owner joins, the space answers for itself; an owner linked while the admin's decision waits must win. 2026-10-25, #120.
- **Who handles a report is the admin's filter `handledBy`, by the spaces verified now** (`ADMIN` an unverified space's, `OWNER` a verified one's), one condition (`handledByWhere`) on the verified spaces' ids `space-links` answers. *Why:* «يعالجه» is the space's verification now, as `space.verified` says, so the filter and the column agree ([finding 167](../architecture/findings/167-the-admins-reports-cannot-be-filtered-by-who-handles-them.md)). 2026-10-29, #131.
- **"Publicly listed" is asked of `spaces` and `lookups` as the space's page asks it** (S12). *Why:* the rule already lives in two queries; a third copy would drift. 2026-10-25, #120.

### Web decisions

- **The chip and the page in the address** (S8): `?status=open|decided&page=`; a value the server would refuse falls back to «الكل»; a change replaces the address and returns to page 1; no default is written. 2026-10-28, #129.
- **«الكل · جديدة · تمت المراجعة», each row's badge saying which decision** (DI-31): the API filters `OPEN | DECIDED` only, so the chips promise no more; «جديد» `info`, «تم التصحيح» `success`, «لم يُعتمد» `neutral`. 2026-10-28, #129.
- **«تعديل الحقل» leads to the field, with no current value shown** (DI-33): the report's reads carry no current value, and showing one would be a second formatter of five fact groups (A10); the prices lead to Packages & prices, the rest to their section of the space profile. 2026-10-28, #129.
- **The owner sees a report's day, never its time** (DI-34, [finding 150](../architecture/findings/150-the-owner-sees-a-reports-exact-time.md)), and the reporter as «زائر» (O12). *Why:* a time beside the desk's check-ins could narrow the reporter to who was present. 2026-10-28, #129.
- **A report with no details says so** (DI-35): «لم يكتب المُبلِّغ تفاصيل.», muted. 2026-10-28, #129.
- **«لم يُعتمد»'s reply is said required before it is sent** (DI-36): the reply's helper says so first; a blank one is focused with its line, nothing sent. 2026-10-28, #129.
- **A decided report is read-only** (DI-37): its decision, its reply or «بلا ردّ», no buttons — a decision is final on the server. 2026-10-28, #129.
- **The toast says where a decided report went** (DI-41): it leaves «جديدة», so the toast names «تمت المراجعة» with «عرض», and says when the reply reached the reporter. 2026-10-28, #129.
- **A chip that matches nothing offers «عرض الكل»** (DI-42), apart from the list that has no report at all. 2026-10-28, #129.
- **The admin's chips are the owner's; the space and the field are fields** (DI-32, P5): the same «الكل · جديدة · تمت المراجعة»; the space a search on the server, the field a select. 2026-10-28, #129.
- **A verified space's report is read-only for the admin, not a dead end** (DI-38): its notice says the owner decides it, with «عرض المساحة» to the space's editor. *Why:* the server refuses the admin's decision there (`can()` with "verified"). 2026-10-28, #129.
- **«يعالجه» per row, from the space's verification now** (DI-39): «الإدارة» or «المالك»; no filter of it on the screen; the API serves one, `handledBy`, since 2026-10-29, #131 ([finding 167](../architecture/findings/167-the-admins-reports-cannot-be-filtered-by-who-handles-them.md)). 2026-10-28, #129.
- **The filters in force are shown as removable tags at every width** (DI-40): a `?space=` link and the phone's sheet would otherwise hide them; `FilterTag` from the layer (finding 8). 2026-10-28, #129.
- **The admin's list stays cards until 1024 px** (DI-43, A7): eight columns need the width; `cardsBelow="lg"`. 2026-10-28, #129.
- **The decision is sent once, with no key** (S4): the same decision again answers the report as it is, so a lost answer is retried from the sheet. 2026-10-28, #129.
- **«بلاغاتي»'s two empty states** (DI-53): none sent yet says how to report, with «تصفّح المساحات»; none under a chip says only that. 2026-10-28, #129.
- **A long message folds by its length, never by measuring** (DI-54): over 160 characters, two lines with «عرض المزيد» (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`), so the toggle is there from the first drawing. 2026-10-28, #129.
- **A chip returns «بلاغاتي» to its first page** (DI-55, S8), the owner's list's address rule reused. 2026-10-28, #129.
- **The details are required for «أخرى» only, said optional otherwise** (DI-44, not A11): the contract requires them there alone, and one rule has one owner. 2026-10-28, #129.
- **The dialog says who reads the report, by the space's verification** (DI-45, U10): the space's management for a verified space, «فريقنا» otherwise, and where the reply will be found. 2026-10-28, #129.
- **A refusal keeps the dialog open with every value** (DI-46): the failure is an alert above the footer; a 429 counts down; no answer says a retry is safe, since a repeat answers the open report. 2026-10-28, #129.
- **The toast says where the reply lands, or that one is already open** (DI-47, DI-48), with «عرض بلاغاتي». *Why:* the repository reads the answer's status — 201 a new report, 200 the open one (S15) — so the user is never told a second report was filed. 2026-10-28, #129.
- **A report is sent once, with no key** (S4): a repeat answers the open report, so a lost answer is retried from the dialog. 2026-10-28, #129.
- **A guest's report waits in memory, never in the address** (S14, DI-60, DI-61): the press holds the space's slug and the page asks them to sign in with the reason; the held report opens the dialog once they are back signed in, and is let go if they close the sign-in, after 15 minutes, or once the guest lands on another page of the site (the shell reports each page it shows: `noteSitePage`), so a later visit signed in opens nothing by itself (#129's review). *Why:* a link carrying the intent would let a crafted link act for a signed-in user. *Cost:* a reload, or a recovery by email, loses it ([finding 168](../architecture/findings/168-a-guests-intent-does-not-survive-a-password-recovery.md)). 2026-10-28, #129.
- **The reply is labelled by who replied** (DI-56): `repliedBy` `SPACE` «ردّ إدارة المساحة», `MASAHA` «ردّ فريقنا» — "our team", never «فريق مساحة», which reads as the space's. A space that left is named as text with its line (C7). 2026-10-28, #129.
