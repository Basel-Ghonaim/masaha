# Spaces

> **Status:** Active · **Class:** Description — what is built, as the code shows it · **Last Updated:** 2026-10-18 · **Owner:** Basel Ghoneim
> **Authority:** the `spaces` capability: a space's profile and its facts (hours and shifts, prices, amenities, contacts), their freshness, its photos, and its life on the platform (created, hidden, deleted, restored), as the admin keeps it. Its endpoints are owned by the [API contract](../api/api-contract.md#spaces-the-admin); its entities and derived values by the [data model](../architecture/data-model.md#spaces). The admin's API, the API-only managed-space routes and what the public [directory](directory.md) reads are built; on the web, the add-space page, the space editor and the row actions of the admin's spaces list. The owner/reception web screen and capacity remain planned.
> **Scope:** the API module `spaces` (L1); `@masaha/shared/spaces`; the web feature `features/spaces`, dashboard-only.

## What it does

- Lets the admin create a space from its profile, its basics and its location; read it; edit the profile and its facts, one group at a time (the hours with the shifts, the prices, the amenities, the contacts), and confirm each group unchanged, as far as [security](../backend/security.md#authorization) allows; hide and show it again; delete it softly and restore it.
- Dates each of the space's fact groups when it is saved or confirmed unchanged, and computes from those dates which groups are stale, which are missing, and when the space was last updated.
- Answers the modules above it: the summaries of a set of spaces, and a page of the admin's spaces list over the ids it is given, of the soft-deleted spaces when asked.
- Answers the [directory](directory.md) the publicly listed spaces over the ids and areas it is given: filtered, searched, ordered and paged, or whole up to a ceiling for the map, each with its week; and a space's public page, read whole, its prices hidden as the public is kept from them.
- Keeps a space's photos for the admin: up to 10, in an order whose first is the cover, each re-encoded on the server as two WebP sizes with no metadata and stored through the storage port; lists them, adds and removes one at a time and orders them, as the [contract](../api/api-contract.md#photos) says; answers [`directory`](directory.md) the covers of a set of spaces, in one query, and a page's photos under its lock.
- On the web, adds a space from its basics and its location, its pin placed on a map; edits a space on a page of its own, its profile, its hours with the shifts, its prices, its amenities and its contacts each saved, dated and confirmed as a section, under the groups still missing, asking before the page is left with unsaved changes, and shows a verified space read-only; and gives each row of the admin's spaces list its actions: edit the space, hide it or show it again, and delete it with an undo, or restore it when it was deleted.

The managed API lets an active owner read and edit the profile and facts, confirm groups and
manage photos; reception can read the complete space and photo response but cannot mutate it.
It reuses the admin services and shapes. The owner/reception web screen and capacity remain
planned; the admin's editor already carries the photo gallery.

## Who uses it

- **The admin,** through the API, the dashboard's add-space page, the space editor, and the row menu of the dashboard's spaces list.
- **`space-links`,** which names the spaces of a user's links from the summaries, and composes the admin's spaces list on the listing page.
- **[`packages`](packages.md),** whose writes take the space's lock first.

## Responsibility boundary

- **Owns** the `spaces` table and its profile, the slug, the hide flag, the soft delete, the fact groups' dates, the staleness rule and the rule of the hidden prices; the hours and the shifts, the prices, the space's amenity links and its own amenities, and its contacts.
- **Leaves** whether a space is verified and its active owners to `space-links`, which the composition root injects ([conventions › Space access](../backend/conventions.md#space-access)); `spaces` never imports `space-links`.
- **Leaves** a space's settings to `space-settings`, the defaults and thresholds to `platform-settings`, and the areas and the amenities, and whether an amenity is active, to [`lookups`](lookups.md).
- **Leaves** who may edit a profile or a fact to `can()` ([security › Authorization](../backend/security.md#authorization)).

## How it composes the platform

- The admin's router sits inside `/admin`, behind the one guard there; the routes on one space first load its links without refusing anyone ([conventions › Space access](../backend/conventions.md#space-access)).
- **The managed routers sit below `/manage/spaces/:spaceId`, behind the one boundary** that admits the space's staff ([security › Authorization](../backend/security.md#authorization)); the shared controllers and services keep the admin's validation, locks and audit names.
- **A new space's settings:** in the creation's transaction, `spaces` reads the new-space defaults from `platform-settings` and writes the space's settings row through `space-settings`, both below it ([conventions › New-space defaults](../backend/conventions.md#new-space-defaults), [data model › Checked by the service](../architecture/data-model.md#checked-by-the-service)).
- Every change is audited by the audit writer, in the change's own transaction ([conventions §6](../backend/conventions.md#6-audit)), with the names the [contract](../api/api-contract.md#spaces-the-admin) gives.
- Staleness reads the platform's thresholds and the one clock ([conventions §11](../backend/conventions.md#11-time)).
- The list's filters are resolved to ids by the module above before `spaces` pages ([conventions §5](../backend/conventions.md#5-pagination)).
- **The web** writes through the transport and TanStack Query; a row action fetches the whole admin scope again, since the list it changes is [`space-links`](space-links.md)' ([architecture §7](../frontend/architecture.md#7-server-state)), while the editor's writes keep the space they answer and only mark the rest of the scope out of date. The page sets the row menu in that list's slot, and the 429's wait above it ([architecture › What a feature exports](../frontend/architecture.md#what-a-feature-exports)); on the add page, it sets the area field of [`lookups`](lookups.md) in the form's slot. The form follows `shared/forms` ([architecture › Forms](../frontend/architecture.md#forms)), preparing its values for the schema, and places the pin with `shared/map`'s point picker ([architecture §6](../frontend/architecture.md#6-map)). The editor's page gives the space's name to the page's title ([architecture §2](../frontend/architecture.md#2-page-groups), *Titles*), and sets in the editor the area field, the amenities' checklist and the area's and the amenities' names of [`lookups`](lookups.md), the way to the lookups' amenities and the space's public page; a write in the editor answers the whole space, which it keeps as the space's state, the rest of the admin scope only marked out of date. The dashboard-only rule keeps it out of the site's download ([architecture §3](../frontend/architecture.md#3-capabilities-features)).

## Behaviour and flows

**Create.** The area must be active, in an active governorate. In one transaction: the slug is chosen, the space is written unverified and shown, its profile dated now and its other fact groups missing, its settings copied from the defaults, and its audit entry written. When the defaults cannot be read, nothing is written. Two creations that chose the same slug at once: the later one tries again with the slugs as they are then, three attempts in all, then answers 409.

**Read and edit.** The space is read with whether it is verified, from its links, so an edit screen can show it editable or read-only; it is read whole under the space's share lock, so a save in progress is never read half done. An edit decides on the links as they are under the space's lock; what it writes and audits is the [contract](../api/api-contract.md#spaces-the-admin)'s.

**Hide, delete, restore.** Each changes the space under its row lock, verified or not; what each answers and leaves behind is the [contract](../api/api-contract.md#spaces-the-admin)'s, and a deleted space's links count for nothing ([space-links](space-links.md#decisions)).

**Adding a space on the web.** `AddSpaceForm` is the add-space page's form, which the spaces list's "Add space" leads to: two sections, *Basics* (the names and the descriptions) and *Location* (the area, the addresses, the landmarks and the pin), then one "Add space".
- **The fields:** each pair in its two languages, the Arabic at the start, each text in its own language and direction; every field that may stay empty is marked "Optional". The area is the field the page hands in, from [`lookups`](lookups.md); it takes the grid's first column.
- **The pin:** a map of the Gaza Strip ([architecture §6](../frontend/architecture.md#6-map)) holds no pin until one is placed; a click places it and a drag moves it. Under it, the pin's coordinates are one value with it: the map writes them, and coordinates typed or pasted ("31.52, 34.45", Arabic digits included) move the pin. They are the keyboard's way to place it, and the way when the map cannot load.
- **Checked in the browser** by the contract's schema: a blank optional field is no value, an empty required one is asked for in the form's own words, and coordinates that name no point, or a point outside the Strip's box, are refused on their field and nothing is sent.
- **Sent once.** The server's field errors land on their fields, the first taking the focus; a 409 (two creations of one name at once) says so and asks to add it again; a 429 counts down and holds the submit.
- **Once created,** the admin's lists are fetched again, the spaces list the page left included, a toast says "{name} added", and the page opens the new space's editor, the space as created already in hand, under its checklist.

**The space editor on the web.** `SpaceEditor` is a space's page in the dashboard, which the page titles by the space's name once it has loaded, «تعديل مساحة» until then. Its loading, its failure (with "Try again") and a space that does not exist or was deleted (the 404) are its own.
- **The frame:** the space's state as two badges, whether it is verified, always, and «مخفية» when hidden; an unverified space's warning that the team's information shows publicly and each section saves alone; the sections; and a note that the space's operating data is private to its management.
- **The sections** are the fact groups, one name each, in the navigation and on their cards. A list of them stays in view beside the sections from 1024 px; below it, a row of chips sticks under the top bar and scrolls sideways. The section in view is marked at every width, and one chosen is marked at once.
- **«المساحة»** is the profile: the basics and the location, the add page's two blocks, under a separator, each keeping its own anchor (`#basics`, `#location`), with one date, one footer and one confirmation.
- **«ساعات العمل»** is the week, Saturday first in both languages, each day «مفتوحة» or «مغلقة» by its switch, its «من» and «إلى» side by side; then the shifts. A time is typed as text, 24-hour in Western digits, left to right in Arabic too; Arabic digits are read, and a time it can read is rewritten as `HH:MM` once its field is left. «إلى» takes 24:00 as a day's end. Hours never saved show the template, Saturday to Thursday 08:00–18:00 and Friday closed, which their first save sends. «نسخ إلى كل الأيام», in the header, copies the first open day's hours to every open day, a closed day staying closed; while no day is open it is disabled, and the line under the header says why. A shift has its Arabic name, its English one, optional, and its two times, stacked below 768 px; up to ten, the limit said beside the disabled "Add"; a saved shift keeps its id. A shift outside every open day is refused on it before anything is sent; one that fits only some open days names, as a helper, the days it does not apply on. A shift a saved price uses cannot be removed, the reason beside its disabled Remove. A removed shift still in use (409), or a shift another admin removed meanwhile (422 on its id), is said in the section, and the space is fetched again.
- **«الأسعار»** is the prices, for display only, the intro under the header saying payment is at the space: from 768 px a small table, its columns named once in its header, and below it a card per price, its fields named on it. Each price is a period «الفترة» (ساعة، يوم، أسبوع، شهر), an audience «لمن» (الجميع، الطلاب), a shift «الوردية» of the space's saved shifts or «بدون وردية», a custom label, its Arabic then its English, both always shown and optional, and an amount in shekels, typed with up to two decimals, Arabic digits and separators read, sent as whole agorot. A new price is a day, for everyone. While the space has no saved shift, every shift field is disabled and one line above the prices says to add shifts first; while «ساعات العمل» has unsaved changes, one line says to save them for a new shift to show. A repeated price is refused on the field that would tell it apart (its label, else its shift, else its period), an amount empty, zero or not an amount on its own, an English label without its Arabic on the Arabic, all before anything is sent. Up to forty, the limit said beside the disabled "Add". A shift another admin removed meanwhile (422 on a price's shift) is said on that field, and the space is fetched again.
- **«المرافق»** is the platform's amenities ticked, in the checklist `lookups` offers, with the way to the lookups' amenities to add one to every space's list, then «مرافق إضافية», the space's own: a row each, its Arabic name and its English one, optional, added up to twenty, the limit said beside the disabled "Add", and removed. An own amenity with no Arabic name, a name too long, or an Arabic name repeated is refused on its row before anything is sent. Both are one group, one save, one date and one confirmation.
- **«التواصل»** is the contacts, a row each, of the contract's seven types, a new one a WhatsApp number. Each value's helper shows the form it is stored in as it is typed, by the shared rule (`normaliseContact`), or an example of its type while it holds none; «هاتف» shows its carrier beside it, read from the stored number's prefix (+97059 جوال, +97056 أوريدو). A value that cannot be stored, an empty one, or one that repeats another once both are stored is refused on its row, in its type's words, before anything is sent. Up to twenty, the limit said as the amenities' is.
- **«الصور»** is the gallery, and no fact group: it carries no date, confirms nothing and is never «تمّت / لم تُضف بعد» in the checklist. The photos come in the server's order, the first marked «الغلاف», and each tile has two icon buttons — «قبل» and «بعد», named for the photo they move, unavailable at the first and the last (marked `aria-disabled`, so the focus stays) — «اجعل الصورة 3 الغلاف» under them on any other tile, named for its photo, and a removal at the end. Nothing moves on screen before the server has accepted: a move, a cover and a removal each send the whole order or the photo's own id, and the gallery is fetched again after the answer; a second move waits until that fetch has landed, so it is never computed from a list the first has changed. A removal asks first, in a dialog that opens on Cancel, its title naming the photo; the dialog closes only once it is gone, then a toast says so by its name, and a failure shows in the open dialog. With no photos the section shows «لا توجد صور بعد» and «الصورة الأولى تصبح الغلاف».
- **Adding photos.** «إضافة صور» opens a picker for many files at once; the files go up **one at a time** in the order chosen, the next starting only after the previous has been answered. Each chosen file has its own tile: the browser reading and shrinking it («جارٍ التحضير…»), then the layer's `Spinner` with its own percentage («جارٍ الرفع 45٪») — there is no progress component in the layer and none is added — then, if it failed, its own line with «إعادة المحاولة» where a re-send of the same bytes could work and «أخرجها من القائمة» always. A file the browser cannot read is refused on its tile with «صيغة غير مدعومة، اختر JPEG أو PNG أو WebP» and **no request is sent**; a refusal the server has already judged (413, 415, 400, 422) offers no retry, because re-sending the same bytes meets it again. Choosing more files than the room left sends what fits and says «رُفعت 3 من 5؛ الحد 10 صور»; at ten, «إضافة صور» is disabled with «الحد 10 صور — احذف صورة لتضيف غيرها» beside it. A 429 fails its own tile, counting down there, and the queue carries on.
- **Leaving while a photo travels.** Photos are immediate actions, so nothing in this section is "unsaved": the only thing leaving loses is an upload still running, and the editor's dialog says exactly that — «تغادر أثناء رفع صورة؟» / «ستفقد أي صورة لم يكتمل رفعها» — naming no section. A move to a section's anchor still never asks, and once the queue has ended the navigation goes on by itself.
- **A refusal stays on the tile it happened to,** read as any refusal is, with its request's reference; a 409 on a move says, in view, that the photos were refreshed, and a 403 or a 404 fetches the space again, so a verified one turns read-only and a deleted one shows its 404. A 404 or a 409 on any gallery write also fetches the gallery again.
- **Each section** shows its group's date («آخر تحديث قبل 3 أيام», in Gaza's days), «قديمة» when the server counts it stale, or «لم تُضف بعد» while it is missing, when it offers Save only. Save stays enabled, except while a wait the server asked for counts down, after a save or a confirmation, when both are held. While its fields differ from the group as saved, it says «تغييرات غير محفوظة» and offers to discard them; while they do not, and the group is saved, it offers «المعلومات ما زالت صحيحة», which renews the date alone. A save of the profile sends only the fields the admin changed since the form was last filled from the space, a cleared one as `null`; the hours, the prices, the amenities and the contacts send their group whole, the hours and the prices judged unchanged against the group as the form was last filled from. A save that changed nothing sends nothing and says how to renew the date, unless the group is missing, whose first save dates it, even empty. A save or a confirmation is said in a toast naming the section, and the section then holds the server's answer; an unchanged section follows the space as it is fetched again. Discard, or a confirmation, clears the line on a save that changed nothing and a save's failure.
- **A refusal stays in its section,** leaving the others as they are: its fields' errors on its fields, the first taking the focus; a 429 counting down; a 403 or a 404 saying the space was verified or deleted meanwhile, and the space fetched again, so a verified one turns read-only and a deleted one shows the 404, whatever was read before. An amenity ticked that the server no longer offers is said in the section, beside any refusal on the rows, and the admin's lists are fetched again, so the checklist marks it.
- **«ما زال ناقصًا»** heads a space with a group never saved: the five groups, each «تمّت» or «لم تُضف بعد», each line a link to its section. It goes once nothing is missing.
- **Leaving** the page with unsaved changes, by a link, Back or the sidebar, asks first, naming the sections, «البقاء» holding the focus; while a save of them is pending, it says they are being saved, and once nothing is left unsaved the navigation goes on. A move to a section's anchor, by a chip or a checklist line, never asks. Closing or reloading the tab asks through the browser.
- **A verified space** shows its values to read under the same sections, its texts each in its own language, the area and the amenities named by `lookups`, its own amenities, its hours, each day, Saturday first, with its range or «مغلقة», and its shifts, its prices, every one with its shift and its label and its amount in shekels, and each contact by its type, as stored, with a phone's carrier, with no section list below 1024 px and no checklist; its notice says its owner edits it, «يعدّلها مالكها», with no name ([finding 59](../architecture/findings/59-the-verified-spaces-editor-cannot-name-its-owner.md)), and leads to its page on the site. Its **«الصور»** is the same gallery in the server's order, with no upload, no move, no cover and no removal drawn at all — the server's `can()` refuses every write on a verified space, and the web's part is that it offers none.

**The row actions on the web.** `SpaceActionsMenu` is a row's actions in the admin's spaces list: for a live space a menu — **Edit**, which leads to the space's editor by the path the page gives, **Hide**, or **Show** for a hidden space, then **Delete**; for a deleted space, **Restore** alone, a button named after the space, since a deleted space has nothing to edit, hide, show or delete. The row's name leads to the editor too ([space-links](space-links.md)).
- **Hide and Show** apply at once. The action stays pending until the list has arrived again, then a toast says what changed, by the space's name.
- **Delete** asks first, in a dialog that opens on Cancel, its title naming the space. Confirmed, the space is deleted; once the list has arrived again, a toast says so with **Undo** for ten seconds, which restores that space, even once its row or the page has gone, and once the list has arrived again a toast says it is restored. Once that restore has settled, the cache keeps no action of the row.
- **Restore**, on a deleted space's row, is the same mutation, the same refresh and the same toasts the Undo uses; it runs once, at once, and the row leaves the list as the space comes back. A space deleted by mistake is therefore restorable long after its toast has gone ([finding 44](../architecture/findings/44-no-screen-restores-a-deleted-space-once-its-toast-has-gone.md)).
- **While an action waits,** the row's menu button, or a deleted row's Restore button, stays, busy, and keeps the focus; its actions wait, but Edit, a way to a page, never does. The dialog returns the focus to that button. A waiting button is marked, not disabled, so the keyboard keeps its place.
- **A failure** shows in a toast that names the action and the space, with the refusal's line and, without a domain code, the request's reference; it stays until it is closed. A 429 on any action, a deleted row's restore and the Undo's included, records until when the server refuses, the later of two; until then every row's actions wait, and `SpaceActionsHold` shows the wait above the list, whatever fails after it. The time is kept apart from the rows, so a change of filter or page keeps the wait, and a page opened during it counts the time left; once it has passed, it is cleared.
- **A name** in a toast or the dialog's title is set apart, marked as English when an English-only name sits in an Arabic sentence; the menu button's name is one string, so its space's name is isolated there instead.
- **The way back from a deleted space to a user** is written, not built: restore the space, unlink its owner, and delete it again ([finding 50](../architecture/findings/50-an-owner-whose-only-spaces-are-deleted-stays-an-owner.md)). There is no web unlink UI in the repository, and none of the three steps reaches a deleted space's links without the restore.

**Freshness.** A group is missing until it is first saved, and stale once its date is older than its threshold, prices by their own ([data model › Derived values](../architecture/data-model.md#derived-values-computed-not-stored)). The admin's "stale or missing" filter keeps both.

**Save a group.** Each group is saved whole, alone, through one save path: under the space's lock, `can()` decides from the links whether the actor may, then the group is replaced, dated now and audited, in one transaction. A save that changes nothing writes nothing, unless the group is missing.

**Hours.** The week and the shifts are saved together. The week is written first, then the shifts change: one named by its id is updated in place, one without an id is created, one left out is removed. A removed shift that a price (or a package, a subscription or a visit) still uses is refused by the database's key, and the transaction takes the week back. Shifts whose Arabic names move between them are first given a passing name no saved shift can hold.

**Prices.** The prices are replaced whole, in their order: nothing refers to a price, so they are written anew. A price may name a shift only of its own space, as the shifts stand once the space's lock is taken, so a shift removed by an hours save meanwhile is refused.

**Amenities.** The group is the platform's amenities the space has, and its own, typed beside them. The set is made exact: the links left out are removed and the new ones added, a kept link untouched. An amenity added must be active, as `lookups` answers in the same transaction; one already linked stays, retired or not. The own amenities are replaced whole, in their order, only when they changed; the same list in another order is a change.

**Contacts.** Each contact arrives validated and in its type's one stored form, from the shared rules both apps run, and the list is replaced whole, in its order.

**Photos.** A space's photos are no fact group: a change dates nothing and is never confirmed. Who may change them is `can()`'s, as for the facts: the admin while the space is unverified, else its owner.
- **An upload** is refused cheaply first, without the lock, when `can()` refuses on the loaded links or the gallery is full, so a refused upload costs no decoding and no store; on the managed route, `can()` is asked before the upload is even counted or read ([security › Authorization](../backend/security.md#authorization)). Then the image core (`shared/images`) decodes it, its content check, and makes the two sizes; both files are stored under a new random key, at once; a failed store removes what was stored and answers 503. Then one transaction: the space's lock, `can()` on the links under it, the count of 10 under it, the row placed last, its audit entry. A refusal or a failure there removes both files before the answer, so two uploads at once can never pass 10.
- **A file left behind** (a crash between the store and the commit, a failed removal) is logged `[storage:orphan]`, and nothing reclaims it in v1 ([finding 63](../architecture/findings/63-a-photos-files-can-outlive-it.md)).
- **A removal** deletes the row and moves the later photos up, with its audit entry, in one transaction under the space's lock; then its files, before the answer. A failed file removal is logged, never answered as a failure. A photo the space does not have writes nothing, and the answer is the same.
- **An order** must name exactly the space's photos, each once, as they are under the lock (409 otherwise); the order the gallery has writes nothing.
- **The list** is read under the space's share lock, as the space's read is.

**Confirm.** A group confirmed unchanged has its date renewed and nothing else, under the space's lock, with its audit entry, on the same path and by the same `can()` rule as a save; a missing group has nothing to confirm (409).

## Decisions

### API decisions

- **The English name is required, the Arabic optional.** *Why:* most spaces are known by an English name. 2026-10-06, #44.
- **The slug comes from the English name,** with the smallest free suffix, deleted spaces counted, and never changes. *Why:* a slug is a public URL, never reused. 2026-10-06, #44.
- **The location is required, inside the Gaza Strip's box with a margin.** *Why:* spaces at the edges must pass. 2026-10-06, #44.
- **Hide, unhide, delete and restore apply to any space,** are audited and safe to repeat; a restore keeps `isHidden`. 2026-10-06, #44.
- **Stale is computed on the server, per fact group,** from the platform's thresholds. *Why:* one rule for the list's filter and each row. 2026-10-06, #44.
- **One profile service serves the admin and, later, the owner;** `can()` decides who may edit, by the rule [security › Authorization](../backend/security.md#authorization) owns. 2026-10-06, #44.
- **A new space is unverified,** with its settings copied from the defaults in the same transaction. 2026-10-06, #44.
- **The landmark is an optional pair of languages.** *Why:* the owner's answer. 2026-10-06, #44.
- **Descriptions keep their line breaks.** *Why:* the create form needs paragraphs. 2026-10-06, #44.
- **"Last update" is the latest of the groups' dates.** 2026-10-06, #44.
- **A group that can be empty is missing until its first save, never fresh while empty** (F2). *Why:* a new space was shown as up to date with no hours, prices, amenities or contacts ([finding 36](../architecture/findings/36-a-new-spaces-empty-fact-groups-count-as-fresh.md)). Spaces that had rows kept their dates. 2026-10-07, #48.
- **"Stale or missing" keeps the missing groups too,** and each row says which are missing. *Why:* both need the admin's attention. 2026-10-07, #48.
- **Every group can be confirmed, the profile included,** each on its own path (D1). *Why:* the edit screen confirms each section, the profile's two among them. 2026-10-07, #48.
- **Confirming a missing group is refused (409);** saving it empty records "none" (D2). *Why:* "still correct" says nothing of a group never entered. 2026-10-07, #48.
- **One `PUT` per group replaces it whole, and each group has its own confirm** (F1). *Why:* each section of the edit screen saves alone; one `PATCH` for every fact, the plan's draft, would date groups the admin never touched. 2026-10-07, #48.
- **The shifts are saved with the hours, and their freshness is the hours'** (F3). *Why:* the design places them there; the data model's "prices (with shifts)" was corrected. 2026-10-07, #48.

- **Contacts are validated per type and stored in one form** (F6), the forms the [data model](../architecture/data-model.md#conventions) gives. *Why:* one number or address has one form, so a repeat is found and a link always opens. 2026-10-07, #48.
- **A network link keeps its path only, but a Facebook profile keeps its numeric `id`** ([data model](../architecture/data-model.md#conventions)). *Why:* a query is mostly tracking, but a profile with no username has no other address (the owner's refinement). 2026-10-07, #48.

- **Only an active amenity can be added; a retired one already linked stays until it is removed** (F7). *Why:* a retired amenity keeps its links (data-model). 2026-10-07, #48.
- **A space keeps amenities of its own beside the platform's, in the amenities group** (the owner's decision, D2): short texts, Arabic required and English optional, in their order, saved, dated, audited and confirmed with the group. *Why:* an owner must be able to say what the space offers beyond the platform's common list, which stays the admin's ([lookups](lookups.md)). 2026-10-12, #64.
- **The own amenities are required in the body, never defaulted to none** (S1). *Why:* the group is saved whole, so a client that leaves them out would empty them unseen. 2026-10-12, #64.

- **An own amenity that names one of the platform's is not refused** (S4). *Why:* an exact match would catch little, since spellings vary, and the save would read the platform's names from `lookups`; a repeat is seen where both lists are shown, as [finding 30](../architecture/findings/30-a-lookups-english-name-is-not-unique.md) reasons. 2026-10-12, #64.

- **Each group's lists and amounts are capped** (D8), at the sizes the [contract](../api/api-contract.md#spaces-the-admin)'s request bodies give. *Why:* bounded forms, and a guard against a slip of the keyboard. 2026-10-07, #48.
- **A new space has no hours until they are saved;** the Saturday–Thursday template is the form's starting point, not data (D9). *Why:* a stored template would date hours nobody entered. 2026-10-07, #48.
- **Every group changes through one save path, the profile's edit included:** the lock, `can()`, the change, the group's date and its audit entry, in one transaction. *Why:* the owner's endpoints (step 5) reuse it (S9). 2026-10-07, #48.

- **The space's read is one consistent state, under the space's lock in share mode** (the owner's choice, S1). *Why:* a read during an hours save could show the old week with the new shifts (finding 43); a lock keeps one isolation level ([ADR 0015](../architecture/decisions/0015-idempotency-and-concurrency.md)), and unlike a snapshot, a test can prove it. 2026-10-11, #60.

- **Photos are no fact group: they carry no date and confirm nothing** (D8). *Why:* the design's gallery has neither; a new photo says nothing of whether the facts are still true. The data model's profile group no longer lists them. 2026-10-13, #77.

- **At most 10 photos, counted under the space's lock; the 11th is a 409 with no code** (D7, the owner's answer). *Why:* two uploads at once must not pass the cap; the gallery disables "add" at 10, so only a race meets it, and the upload's only 409 needs no code. 2026-10-13, #77.
- **Files are stored before the transaction and removed when it refuses or fails; no reclamation job in v1** (D9). *Why:* no network call inside a transaction, no work after the answer ([finding 63](../architecture/findings/63-a-photos-files-can-outlive-it.md)). 2026-10-13, #77.
- **Removing a photo the space does not have writes nothing, and answers as a removal** (S4). *Why:* the gallery's actions are immediate, and a removal retried after a lost answer must not show a failure; the owners' unlink set the precedent. 2026-10-13, #77.
- **An order answers 204, like the lookups' order** (S9). *Why:* the gallery sent the order it shows. 2026-10-13, #77.
- **A refused upload is refused before its work** (S1): `can()` and the count, without the lock, then decided again under it. *Why:* a verified space's upload or an 11th photo would cost a decode and two stores. 2026-10-13, #77.
- **The gallery has its own read, `GET …/photos`** (D6). *Why:* `AdminSpace`, which the web's editor is built on, does not change. 2026-10-13, #77.
- **Photos have a `can()` action of their own, `space.photos.manage`, under the facts' rule** (S3). *Why:* the table names what it decides, and photos are no fact group. 2026-10-13, #77.
- **Each change is audited with the gallery's ids before and after, never a key or a URL** (D10). *Why:* the gallery is recorded whole, as every group is. 2026-10-13, #77.
- **The public listing is one query over `spaces` and its facts, with the filters of other modules given as ids** ([directory › Decisions](directory.md#decisions)). *Why:* every filter applies before the page is cut; the search's score and the orders are computed in it. 2026-10-13, the directory item.
- **The hidden-price rule is `spaces`', with "verified" as an input:** an unverified space's stale prices are hidden on its page, and the same spaces are left out of both price filters, in the query. *Why:* the staleness is this capability's; "verified" is `space-links`', resolved first. 2026-10-13, the directory item.
- **The public page reads the space as the admin's read does,** under its share lock, and a hidden space reads as none. *Why:* a save in progress is never shown half done (finding 43). 2026-10-13, the directory item.
- **The admin's space read receives active owners composed from `space-links`** (D1). *Why:* `spaces` never imports `space-links` (an L2 capability); the composition root injects an `ownersFor` reader so the single-space read and fact writes carry active owner names under the existing space share lock. 2026-10-15, #82.
- **The managed photo writes ask `can()` at the route, through `requireCan`, before the upload is counted or its body read;** the service's own `can()`, under the space's lock, still decides (the owner's D4). *Why:* reception's refused upload was read into memory first, and the managed reads' two copies of the `space.read` check needed one home: the boundary, which is that check. 2026-10-17, #86.
- **Reception reads the space whole, its owners' names and ids included** (the owner's D5, the review's option A). *Why:* the staff who work the space every day know its owner, and a reception-only shape would be a second contract and a rule no lane tests. Revisit when staff management (step 5b) lands. 2026-10-17, #86.

### Web decisions

- **The web's row actions are this capability's,** set by the page in the admin's list's slot ([space-links](space-links.md#decisions)) (L1). *Why:* hiding and deleting a space are its life on the platform; the list is `space-links`' read. 2026-10-07, #50.
- **An admin's row action on a space fetches the whole admin scope again,** and stays pending until it has arrived; the editor's writes do not (S3, below). *Why:* the list it changes is another capability's, whose keys this one does not name; the front desk refreshes a space's scope the same way ([architecture §7](../frontend/architecture.md#7-server-state)). 2026-10-07, #50.
- **Each action ends in a toast that names its space:** hidden, shown again, deleted with an Undo (L2), and restored once the Undo succeeds (the owner's alternative 2). *Why:* the design's feedback, a first for the admin's screens, the lookups keeping none; the restored toast confirms the Undo even once the page has changed. 2026-10-07, #50.
- **The Undo restores that space for ten seconds,** even once its row or the page has gone. *Why:* long enough to reach from the keyboard; the deleted spaces' list is the way to restore one later, as the decision below records. 2026-10-07, #50.
- **Delete asks first, and its line says only what is true:** "It leaves the site and the list; you’ll find it under «Deleted» to restore it." *Why:* the design's "can be restored" holds only through the toast, and the place it is restored from is now a screen of its own. It replaced the line that promised only the toast's Undo (#84's review). The confirmation is destructive, as the design system marks a removal. 2026-10-07, #50; amended 2026-10-17, #85.
- **A failure's toast names the action and the space, and stays until it is closed;** while a 429 counts down on any row, every row's actions wait, and the wait shows above the list (L5). *Why:* a table has no room for a failure in its row, a toast outlives the row a delete removes, and a disabled item shows its reason beside it ([foundation §10](../frontend/design-system/foundation.md#10-accessibility-baseline)). 2026-10-07, #50.
- **A 429's wait is one time, kept in the query cache under the admin's scope (`['admin', 'spaces', 'hold']`),** set by any action's 429 to now plus its wait, the later of two, read as the time left, and cleared once it has passed; each action still leaves the cache with its row (`gcTime: 0`). *Why:* the server holds the window, and the cache keeps its last answer ([architecture › Where state lives](../frontend/architecture.md#where-state-lives)), cleared with the rest of the user's data when the session ends. Read from the failed actions, the wait lifted as soon as a change of filter or page unmounted their rows, and a page opened later counted the whole wait again (the owner's decision after #50's review). 2026-10-08, #50.
- **A space's name in a toast or the dialog's title carries its language;** in the menu button's name, a string, it is isolated. *Why:* an English-only name in an Arabic sentence ([localisation › Content in two languages](../frontend/localisation.md#content-in-two-languages)). 2026-10-07, #50.
- **No owners' items in the row menu** (L7). *Why:* no entry point leads nowhere. "Add space" arrived with its page (#57), "Edit" with the editor (#74). 2026-10-07, #50.
- **A space is added in one form with one submit:** its basics and its location, sent as one creation (D1). *Why:* a space does not exist before it is created, so the design's save per section belongs to the edit screen. 2026-10-10, #57.
- *Superseded 2026-10-13 by #74's «Once added, the page opens the new space's editor», below.* **Once added, the page returns to the list, and a toast names the space** (D2). The creation fetches the admin's lists the add page left, and waits for them, so the list opens already holding the new space. *Why:* as #50's actions say what they did; the edit screen, where the facts follow, is not built, so the list is where the new space is seen; a list fetched only once shown would first show its old rows (#57's review). 2026-10-10, #57.
- **The two sections are this capability's, made for the edit screen to reuse,** with nothing only the edit would use (D3). *Why:* the edit screen shows the same fields, each section saved alone. 2026-10-10, #57.
- **The area is one active area, from the field `lookups` offers,** which the page sets in the form (D4). *Why:* the areas are that capability's fact, and no feature imports another ([architecture § What a feature exports](../frontend/architecture.md#what-a-feature-exports)). 2026-10-10, #57.
- **Field errors land on their fields, checked first in the browser by the contract's schema; a 409 is said plainly, a 429 counts down** (D5). *Why:* one rule in both apps, and the general conflict line's "refresh" would lose the form. 2026-10-10, #57.
- **The pin is placed on a real map, and has no place until the admin gives it one** (D6, D7). *Why:* a pin set by default would pass unseen, wrong. 2026-10-10, #57.
- **The coordinates are a field of their own, one value with the pin,** and read Arabic digits and the Arabic comma. *Why:* the keyboard's way to place the pin, the way when the map cannot load (D8), and how an admin pastes a place from a map application. 2026-10-10, #57.
- **Every field that may stay empty says so; the landmark is asked in both languages,** like the addresses. *Why:* the design marked the landmark alone, and showed one landmark where the contract has two (design issues 1 and 2). 2026-10-10, #57.
- **The page's title is in the top bar, with a trail back to the list; "Add space" sits above the list, at its end, at every width.** *Why:* the design drew the title twice, and the button in two places; on a phone the top bar is full (design issues 5 and 7). 2026-10-10, #57.
- **The editor is a page per space, titled by the space's name** through the one record title the top bar and the tab read. *Why:* the record-name rule of [architecture §2](../frontend/architecture.md#2-page-groups), built here for its first page. 2026-10-13, #74.
- **The editor's sections are this capability's, with no interface only the admin's page uses;** the page sets what differs, and the sections call the admin's endpoints, the only ones the API has. *Why:* the owner edits the same groups (O1). 2026-10-13, #74.
- **One section per fact group, each saved, dated and confirmed alone;** a group never saved offers Save only, and the confirmation shows only while a saved section is unchanged (A1). *Why:* the API saves and confirms one group at a time, and a missing group has nothing to confirm. 2026-10-13, #74.
- **The profile's save sends only the fields the admin changed since the form was last filled from the space,** a cleared optional one as `null` (S1). *Why:* resent whole, an unchanged area hidden since would be refused, and the section could never be saved ([finding 60](../architecture/findings/60-the-area-field-shows-no-area-for-a-space-whose-area-was-hidden-since.md)); nor is a field another admin changed meanwhile sent back. 2026-10-13, #74.
- **A save that changes nothing sends nothing,** and the section says how to renew the date (S2, design issue 2). *Why:* the server writes nothing and keeps the date, so a toast saying "saved" would be untrue. 2026-10-13, #74.
- **A write's answer is the space's state:** kept as the space's query, the rest of the admin scope only marked out of date (S3). *Why:* the editor shows the answer itself; the list is fetched again when it is shown. It departs from the row actions' wait for the list, which they change on screen. 2026-10-13, #74.
- **A 403 or a 404 on a write fetches the space again** (S4), and a space found deleted shows the 404 even when it was read before. *Why:* an owner linked, or a delete, while the page was open: the page then shows the space as it is, a verified one read-only, a deleted one gone. 2026-10-13, #74.
- **Leaving with unsaved changes asks first,** naming the sections; a move within the page never asks. The guard is this capability's, no other form asking yet (S8). **A save that lands while it asks lets the navigation go on,** and while one is pending it says the changes are being saved (the owner's choice at #74's review). *Why:* nothing is left to lose, and a save in flight is not lost. **A section's link is the router's** (#74's review). *Why:* a plain fragment link could have its scroll undone by the router's scroll restoration. 2026-10-13, #74.
- **The section in view is read from where the sections cross the window,** a section chosen marked at once (S9); below 1024 px the row of chips sticks under the top bar, which keeps one line (S7, design issue 4). 2026-10-13, #74.
- **The platform's amenities are ticked in the checklist `lookups` offers** ([its rule for a retired amenity](lookups.md#behaviour-and-flows); S5, design issue 3). *Why:* the amenities are that capability's list. A ticked amenity retired meanwhile is said in the section, whatever else is refused, and the admin's lists are fetched again (#74's review). 2026-10-13, #74.
- **A phone's carrier is a display rule of the web,** read from its stored form's prefix (S6). *Why:* the API never computes it, and the stored form stays the shared rule's; no other capability shows it yet. 2026-10-13, #74.
- **A contact's helper shows its stored form as it is typed, by the shared rule** (AD-006). *Why:* the admin sees what will be kept, and the browser refuses what the server would, with no copy of the rule. A repeat is found only once every contact can be stored, as the server's schema finds it. 2026-10-13, #74.
- **Once added, the page opens the new space's editor, under its checklist; the toast stays** (the owner's A2). It supersedes #57's «Once added, the page returns to the list». The creation fetches no cached editor again. *Why:* the editor is where the facts the new space still lacks are entered; until it existed, the list was where the new space was seen. The space created is kept as the editor's, and the admin's lists are still fetched before the page moves on, so the list shows the new space when the admin returns to it. 2026-10-13, #74.
- **The way into the editor is the row's Edit and the row's name** (the owner's prompt). *Why:* a row's name is what the admin looks for; Edit, a way to a page, never waits on a 429. 2026-10-13, #74.
- **Each toast names its section** (design issue 1). *Why:* with several sections, «حُفظ القسم» said nothing of which. 2026-10-13, #74.
- *Superseded 2026-10-13 by #79's «Every checklist line leads to its section», below.* **The checklist lists the five groups; a line leads to its section only once the editor draws it** (design issue 5). *Why:* the editor draws no section for the hours or the prices, and a link must lead somewhere. 2026-10-13, #74.

- **A 429 fails its own tile and the queue carries on,** each tile counting down its own wait. *Why:* each file's failure is its own by D3, and pausing the queue would need a second timer rule and a new string. 2026-10-17, #85.
- **The gallery does not write the admin list's 429 hold key.** *Why:* that key is that list's own wait, above that screen; a refusal this section shows itself belongs here. 2026-10-17, #85.
- **The gallery's uploads go through `shared/uploads`, not a new method on `api`.** *Why:* shrinking and sending a file is a platform mechanism an avatar or another file kind reuses, and `api.post` already takes the config `onUploadProgress` needs, so the transport is not touched at all. 2026-10-17, #85.
- **An upload has its own 120-second timeout, and leaving aborts the one in flight.** *Why:* the client's 15 seconds cover the whole request, upload included, and on the weak links this is built for a shrunk photo needs longer; the Leave in the dialog must really stop the queue, or its promise that an unfinished photo is lost would be false. 2026-10-17, #85.
- **A removal closes its dialog only on success, and a move takes one write at a time until the gallery is fetched again.** *Why:* a failure the admin cannot see is no answer, and a move computed from a list the server has already changed would be saved silently. 2026-10-17, #85.
- **«رُفعت N من M» is written when the queue ends, from what went up,** and cleared when the next choice starts. *Why:* at choice time it could only predict, and it said a photo went up that a later failure had refused. 2026-10-17, #85.
- **The gallery's failures are read through `useRefusalView`**, each pending tile with its own, so each counts down its own wait. *Why:* one reader for every refusal, and the tile needs the reference and the countdown for nothing more. 2026-10-17, #85.
- **The leave guard asks while a photo is travelling,** and says only that an unfinished photo will be lost. *Why:* photos are immediate actions, so nothing there is unsaved, and the dialog must name what is really at stake and no section that has nothing unsaved (D6). 2026-10-17, #85.
